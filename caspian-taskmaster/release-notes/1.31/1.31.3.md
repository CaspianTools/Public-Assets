## Full changes

### Changed
- **New screenshots on the Marketplace.** The old ones showed a board with a single "Test" card and views that have since changed or been removed. The new gallery has 13 current views in dark and light themes: the board, table, issue popup (details, dependencies, comments, audit log, Claude chat), new issue with AI triage and duplicate warning, filter drawer, sprints, settings, metrics, and the sidebar beside your code. The specs page uses them too.

### Added
- **`npm run screenshots`** retakes every screenshot from the real packaged extension, against a fictional demo project, in dark and light themes. Screenshots now stay current by rerunning one command instead of capturing them by hand.
