**Shipped v1.113.1** · 2026-07-03

A quick follow-up to the new language switcher: the **About** and **Privacy Policy** pages are now fully translated too. The pages that explain what Caspian Office does — and exactly how it handles your data — read in your language, right-to-left included for Arabic.

- 🌍 **Improved — About & Privacy translated:** the full text of both pages (all 12 languages) now switches with the header globe, alongside the menu, search and tool names shipped in v1.113.0.
- 📬 **Fixed — shared contact address:** the contact and security-report email now points to a shared team inbox (`caspiantools@googlegroups.com`) instead of a personal address.

Still English for now: the Updates version-checker and the Changelog history (utility/log surfaces) — and the insides of each individual tool, which are the next translation wave.
