A tidier Typing tutor: the keyboard-layout and difficulty-level pickers now live in the Settings window, leaving the main screen to your stats, the passage and the finger-guide keyboard.

- imp — **Pickers moved into Settings (⚙).** Keyboard layout (English · العربية · Azərbaycanca · Русский) and difficulty level are now two labelled groups inside Settings; picking one starts a fresh passage right behind the window.
- new — **At-a-glance summary line.** A compact line under the stats shows the active layout and level (e.g. *Русский · Beginner — …*) — click it to jump straight into Settings.
