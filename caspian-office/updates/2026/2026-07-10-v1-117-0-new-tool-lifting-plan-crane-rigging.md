**Shipped v1.117.0** · 2026-07-10

New HSE tool — **Lifting plan** — for planning a crane/rigging lift with the maths done for you and a live sketch of the lift. An indicative planning aid, 100% client-side and offline.

- 🆕 **New — Lifting plan.** Enter the load, the number of sling legs and the sling angle, list your rigging accessories with their Working Load Limits, and get the **gross lifted load**, the **per-leg sling tension** (from the load-angle factor 1 ÷ sin θ), the **crane-capacity utilisation** and a **WLL pass/fail check** on every item — colour-banded green / amber / red — next to a live **sketch of the lift** (single sling, up to four legs, or a spreader beam). Works in metric or imperial; export the sketch as PNG or SVG, or print the whole plan sheet as a PDF. A competent-person disclaimer makes clear it is an indicative planning aid, not a lift plan, and every value must be verified against manufacturer charts and LOLER 1998 / BS 7121 / ASME B30.
