**Shipped v1.118.0** · 2026-07-10

Three ways to move faster through the toolkit — plus durable, on-device saving for the board tools.

- 🆕 **Command palette** — press **Ctrl / ⌘ + K** anywhere to open a search overlay and jump straight to any tool. When it's empty it shows your favourites and most-used. The header search box and the `/` shortcut still work as before.
- ✨ **Typo-tolerant search** — every search now forgives misspellings and partial words: "compres" finds Compress PDF, "mearge" finds Merge PDF — in the palette, the header box and the home page.
- 🆕 **Pin your favourites** — tap the ★ on any tool card to pin it to a quick-access row at the top of the home page. Kept on your device only.
- 🆕 **Save boards to a file** — the Lean/CI boards (Kaizen, PDCA, DMAIC, ICE, PICK, CI board) and the Business Model Canvas can now link to a `.json` file on your device and autosave to it as you work — so your progress survives a cleared browser and moves between machines. Fully optional; nothing is uploaded, and browsers without the feature keep the existing manual export.
- 🐛 **Housekeeping** — corrected the update-check URL shown on the About & Privacy pages, and tightened the site security policy so it stays consistent with what the app actually loads.
