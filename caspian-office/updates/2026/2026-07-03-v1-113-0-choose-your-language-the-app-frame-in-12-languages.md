**Shipped v1.113.0** · 2026-07-03

Caspian Office now speaks your language. A new **globe switcher in the header** translates the whole app frame — the menu, search, home page, footer and **every tool's name & description** — and remembers your choice on the device. It's 100% client-side, works offline, and adds no AI and no tracking.

- 🌍 **New — UI language switcher across 12 languages:** English, العربية (Arabic), 中文 (Chinese), Español, Português, Français, Deutsch, Русский, 日本語, Bahasa Indonesia, Türkçe and Azərbaycan. Your pick is saved locally and applied every visit.
- ↔️ **New — right-to-left support:** choosing **Arabic** flips the whole app frame to read right-to-left.
- 🧭 **Improved — translated navigation:** browse and find tools in your language (search still matches English too). Each tool's own screens stay in English **for now** — translating the insides of the tools, and the in-app help, are the next waves.

Built the vanilla way: a tiny bundled message catalog, no framework, fully inlined so language switching even works in the offline single-file download.
