**Shipped v1.119.0** · 2026-07-10

The **Incident Rate Calculator** now covers the full family of HSE safety performance rates — enter your case counts, fatalities, near misses and hours worked, and every rate updates live on the OSHA (200,000) or ILO (1,000,000) base.

- **Improved — six more safety rates.** Alongside TRIR, DART, LTIFR/LTIR and severity rate, it now works out the **Fatal Accident Rate (FAR)**, the **All Injury / Accident Frequency Rate (AIFR/AFR)**, the **near-miss frequency rate**, and separate **medical-treatment** and **first-aid** case rates.
- **New — TRCF/TRIFR made explicit.** The recordable-rate card now reads **TRIR · TRCF/TRIFR** — the same figure is TRIR on the OSHA 200,000 base and TRCF/TRIFR on the ILO 1,000,000 base. FAR always uses its fixed 100-million-hour base.
- **Fixed — tidier results.** The two identical LTIFR and LTIR tiles are merged into one **LTIFR · LTIR** card, making room for the new rates.

Still 100% client-side, no-AI, works offline. Your figures never leave your device.
