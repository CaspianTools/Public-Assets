**Shipped v1.113.4** · 2026-07-04

The everyday calculators now read entirely in your language — inside and out.

- 🌍 **Improved — 7 more tools localized:** **Percentage**, **Tip & bill split**, **Sales tax**, **GPA**, **Age**, **Time duration**, and the **Scientific & unit calculator** are now fully translated — their controls, live results and the "About this tool" guide — in all 12 languages, right-to-left for Arabic. Switch language while one is open and the results re-translate on the spot.

That completes the whole **Everyday Calculators** collection (with the discount calculator from v1.113.3). More collections follow.
