**Shipped v1.116.1** · 2026-07-09

Seven more tools now read in your language, *inside* — the heavier AI & document tools, fully translated into all 12 languages, right-to-left for Arabic. Still 100% client-side, no-AI, works offline.

- **Improved — AI & document tools localized.** **Image-to-text (OCR)**, **speech-to-text** dictation, **text-to-speech**, the **slides** builder, the **word processor**, the **text editor** and the **spreadsheet converter** now speak your language throughout — labels, buttons, live status/progress and the "About this tool" guides — in all 12 languages including Arabic (RTL).

That brings the "translated inside" count to **96 tools**.
