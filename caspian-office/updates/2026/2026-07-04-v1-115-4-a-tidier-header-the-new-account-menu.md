**Shipped v1.115.4** · 2026-07-04

A small fix with a nice side-effect: the top bar no longer overflows on phones, and there's a new **Account** menu.

- 🐛 **Fixed:** on narrow screens the header had grown too crowded (brand · Tools · notifications · settings · language · Download), and the red **Download** button was getting clipped off the right edge.
- 🆕 **Account menu:** a **person icon at the far right** now opens a menu with **Settings** — and, on mobile, **Updates** and **Language** too. On phones those standalone icons fold into this menu, so the header stays tidy and everything is still one tap away.
- 🖥️ On desktop nothing moves — the notifications bell and language switcher stay in the bar; the Account menu is a quick shortcut to your Settings (which hold your "My details" profile).
