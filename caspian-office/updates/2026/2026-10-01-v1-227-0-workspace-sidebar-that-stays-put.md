**Shipped v1.227.0** · 2026-10-01

The Workspace sidebar keeps its place. Opening a tool no longer scrolls the sidebar away from All tools, and your favourite and recent tools are always one click away.

- **Fixed — opening a tool no longer moves the sidebar.** A tool picked in the sidebar leaves the sidebar where it was. A tool opened from a link, search or the overview is brought into view only when it is out of sight.
- **New — pinned top block.** All tools, Favourites (with your pinned tools listed), Recently used (your last five) and Collections stay fixed above the tool list.
- **Improved — sections fold one at a time** under a sticky header, and there is a new Collapse all button.
- **Improved — smaller touches.** Hovering a shortened name shows the full name, the open tool has an accent bar, the footer reads "Offline · private", the version opens the changelog, and sidebar search offers "See all results". In all 12 languages.
