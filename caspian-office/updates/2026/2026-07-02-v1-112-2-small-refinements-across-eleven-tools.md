**Shipped v1.112.2** · 2026-07-02

A round of small refinements across eleven tools — each verified with a browser scan and functional interaction tests.

**⚡ Improved**
- **Age calculator** shows exact hours (not a days×24 estimate)
- **UUID** count arrows regenerate live; **decision-matrix** scores clamp to your chosen scale
- **Break-even** shows the exact figure beside the rounded one; **JSA** gets a **Clear** button
- **Sticky notes** no longer pile up on the same spot; the **habit tracker** remembers the month you were viewing
- **Colour-palette → Random** now gives properly varied palettes

**🛠 Fixed**
- **OCR** "copy" now works when the app is opened offline as a single file (with a fallback)
- **SWOT** undo no longer records a step per keystroke
- **OG-card** template preview matches each design's gradient angle
