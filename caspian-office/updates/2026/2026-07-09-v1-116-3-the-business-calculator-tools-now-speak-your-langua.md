**Shipped v1.116.3** · 2026-07-09

Ten more tools now read in your language, *inside* — the business documents and calculators, fully translated into all 12 languages, right-to-left for Arabic. Still 100% client-side, no-AI, works offline.

- **Improved — Business & calculator tools localized.** The **quote generator**, **resume/CV builder**, **email-signature maker**, **invoice builder**, **budget planner**, **break-even**, **BMI**, **graphing calculator**, **periodic table** and **statistics** now speak your language throughout — form labels, printable invoice/quote headings, BMI categories, statistic labels, the periodic-table legend and the "About this tool" guides — in all 12 languages including Arabic (RTL). Element names, currency codes, maths and your own data stay as-is.

That brings the "translated inside" count to **118 tools** — only ~35 to go.
