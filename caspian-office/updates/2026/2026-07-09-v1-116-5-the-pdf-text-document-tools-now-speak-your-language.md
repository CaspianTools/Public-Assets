**Shipped v1.116.5** · 2026-07-10

Four more tools now read in your language, *inside* — the PDF text & document tools, fully translated into all 12 languages, right-to-left for Arabic. Still 100% client-side, no-AI, works offline.

- **Improved — PDF text & document tools localized.** The **PDF metadata editor**, **PDF → JSON**, **PDF → text** and **Word → PDF** now speak your language throughout — controls, metadata field labels, extraction status/progress and the "About this tool" guides — in all 12 languages including Arabic (RTL). Your files and their contents stay on your device, untouched.

That brings the "translated inside" count to **128 tools** — **every registered module tool is now done.** Only the inline base PDF & image tools remain (~24), coming next.
