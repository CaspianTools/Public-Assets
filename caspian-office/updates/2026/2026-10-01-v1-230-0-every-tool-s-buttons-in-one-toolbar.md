**Shipped v1.230.0** · 2026-10-01

Every tool's buttons in one toolbar. In the Workspace, Download, the tool's other buttons, undo/redo and its heart now sit together in a toolbar at the right edge, joined by new Share, Guide and Full screen buttons; the top bar keeps only navigation and the app's own controls.

- **New — one toolbar for the open tool**, fixed mid-way down the right edge; below 1100px wide it moves to the bottom, centred under the tool (one row on phones), so the tool keeps its full width.
- **New — Share, Guide and Full screen.** Share by link, QR code or your device's share sheet; open the tool's how-to page; show the tool on its own without the sidebar and top bar (Esc brings them back).
- **Fixed — menus stay on screen.** The Download menu opens beside the toolbar and is kept inside the window, including on phones and on the site.
- **Fixed — smaller things:** the toolbar no longer covers wide tools on 900–1100px screens, "/" on a tablet goes to search again, and reduced motion stops the phone menu sliding.
