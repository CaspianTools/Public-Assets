**Shipped v1.116.8** · 2026-07-10 · 🎉 **the localization wave is complete**

Every tool now speaks your language, *inside*. This release localizes the final eight built-in tools — and with them, **all 152 tools** in Caspian Office now read in all 12 languages, right-to-left for Arabic. Still 100% client-side, no-AI, works offline.

- **Improved — the i18n wave is complete (152/152).** The last eight built-in tools now read in your language too: **Sign PDF**, **PDF forms**, **Redact**, **Edit PDF**, **Image compress**, **Image resize**, **Image crop** and the **QR-code generator**. Every control, option, status message and in-app guide across the whole toolkit is now translated.

**What "translated inside" means:** not just menu names — the actual working screens. Buttons, drop-zones, options, live status and the "About this tool" guides, in **العربية · 中文 · Español · Português · Français · Deutsch · Русский · 日本語 · Bahasa Indonesia · Türkçe · Azərbaycan · English**. Arabic renders right-to-left. Your files and data never leave your device.

Switch languages any time with the 🌐 globe in the header.
