**Shipped v1.157.0** · 2026-07-19

Every interactive diagram tool now shares **one consistent canvas**. No more per-tool toolbars that had quietly drifted apart — Flowchart, Mind map, Org chart, 5 Whys, Fishbone, Issue tree, Bowtie and User flow all look and work the same way.

- **imp** · **Standardised canvas toolbars.** A **top-left toolbox** (undo · redo · upload · load sample · reset) and a **bottom-right toolbox** (zoom out · zoom % · zoom in · centre · full screen) — icon-only with tooltips, one matching icon set across every tool.
- **imp** · **Plain white canvas.** The dotted grid (and its toggle) is gone — every canvas is a clean white surface, the same in light and dark mode.
- **new** · **Import your saved diagrams.** Every canvas can now **upload a JSON** it exported, and any tool missing a **JSON download** gained one in the Download menu, so your work round-trips. The **User flow maker** also gains a **full-screen** button.
