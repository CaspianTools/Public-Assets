**Shipped v1.108.1** · 2026-07-01

Connecting shapes and naming them in the **User flow maker** are both much easier now.

- **Fixed** — Dragging an arrow between shapes works again. The blue link dots were tiny, hover-only, and (thanks to an SVG hover-scale that grew from the wrong origin) lunged away from the cursor. They're now bigger, have a generous grab zone, stay put, and appear the moment you hover or select a shape.
- **Improved** — Name shapes right on the canvas. A new shape opens ready to type its label, and you can click a selected shape (or double-click any shape) to rename it in place — leave it blank for an unlabelled shape.
