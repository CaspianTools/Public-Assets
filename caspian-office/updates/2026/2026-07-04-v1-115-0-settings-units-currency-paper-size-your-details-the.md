**Shipped v1.115.0** · 2026-07-04

Caspian Office now has a **Settings page** — set your preferences once and every tool starts with your choice, instead of asking again in each tool. Everything is saved privately on your device; nothing is uploaded.

- 🆕 **A Settings page** — reach it from the **gear** in the header or at **/settings**.
- 🆕 **Units (metric/imperial)** — the BMI, heat-index and all eight drilling calculators follow one global choice; flip it in any tool and the rest come along.
- 🆕 **Default currency** — new invoices, quotes and other money tools start in your currency (symbol only, no exchange rates).
- 🆕 **Paper size (A4 / US Letter)** — used by PDF and print exports across the document and board tools.
- 🆕 **"My details"** — save your name, business, contact and logo once to prefill the invoice, quote, business-card and email-signature tools.
- 🆕 **Light / dark theme** — light, dark, or follow your system. Dark paints the whole app frame; tools stay on a clean light page for now.
