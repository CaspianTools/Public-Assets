**Shipped v1.116.4** · 2026-07-10

Six more tools now read in your language, *inside* — the Lean / Continuous-Improvement boards, fully translated into all 12 languages, right-to-left for Arabic. Still 100% client-side, no-AI, works offline.

- **Improved — Lean & Continuous-Improvement boards localized.** The **PDCA**, **DMAIC**, **Kaizen** and **CI** boards, plus **ICE scoring** and the **PICK chart**, now speak your language throughout — column headings (Plan/Do/Check/Act, Define/Measure/Analyze/Improve/Control…), card fields (owner, due, impact, effort), the ICE score columns, the PICK quadrants and the "About this tool" guides — in all 12 languages including Arabic (RTL). Your card content stays exactly as you typed it.

That brings the "translated inside" count to **124 tools** — only ~28 to go (the PDF & image tools).
