**Shipped v1.115.10** · 2026-07-09

Ten more tools now read in your language, *inside* — every control, status message and help guide translated into all 12 languages, right-to-left for Arabic. Still 100% client-side, no-AI, works offline.

- **Improved — Image & media tools localized.** The **image converter**, **image editor**, **background remover**, **watermark**, **EXIF viewer**, **scene extractor**, **chart maker**, **barcode generator**, **video-to-GIF** and the **shade generator** now speak your language throughout — labels, buttons, live status and the "About this tool" guides — in all 12 languages including Arabic (RTL).

That brings the "translated inside" count to **89 tools**.
