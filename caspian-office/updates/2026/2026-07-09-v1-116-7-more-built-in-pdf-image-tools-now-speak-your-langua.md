**Shipped v1.116.7** · 2026-07-10

Eight more built-in PDF & image tools now speak your language *inside*, fully translated into all 12 languages, right-to-left for Arabic. Still 100% client-side, no-AI, works offline.

- **Improved — More built-in PDF tools localized.** **Images → PDF**, **PDF → images**, **Watermark**, **Compress**, **Crop**, **Scan to PDF**, **HTML → PDF** and **Compare PDFs** now read in your language throughout — drop-zones, options, toolbars and status messages — in all 12 languages including Arabic (RTL). Everything runs on your device.

That brings the "translated inside" count to **144 tools** — just 8 built-in PDF & image tools to go.
