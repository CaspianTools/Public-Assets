**Shipped v1.155.0** · 2026-07-18

Every tool's "About this tool" area now ends with a **Co-Authors** section, right alongside the FAQ and tips — a small, permanent place to credit the people who help make each tool better.

- **New — Co-Authors on every tool.** When a tool credits contributors, their names show up here as chips. Until then, a subtle note invites you to help make Caspian Office better and get your name featured in this section.
- Works across all 12 UI languages — the heading and the invite are translated, while contributor names stay exactly as written in every language.
- In-app only and 100% client-side, as always: no accounts, no tracking, no AI.
