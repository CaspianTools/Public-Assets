**Shipped v1.142.0** · 2026-07-17

Search-engine plumbing release: every crawl request now lands perfectly, and every page is discoverable without running a line of JavaScript.

- **fix** Old tool addresses from the June URL clean-up (`/tool/split/`, `/tool/counter/`, …) now 301 straight to the tool's current page — 32 permanent redirects, single hop each.
- **imp** The home page carries a complete crawlable catalogue of every tool, collection and converter (invisible in the app); collection pages list all member tools; every tool page shows a Home › Collection › Tool breadcrumb with matching JSON-LD.
- **imp** The sitemap now lists only final canonical URLs (198) with real per-page last-modified dates.
