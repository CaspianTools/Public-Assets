**Shipped v1.116.0** · 2026-07-09

The **Whiteboard** now goes true full screen on any device. Tapping **full screen** expands the board edge-to-edge on portrait phones, landscape phones and desktop — like a proper drawing app — instead of shrinking to a thin strip with wasted space around it.

- 🔧 **Improved — Whiteboard fills the whole screen.** A single uniform scale keeps your drawings undistorted and the whole board visible, and the extra space becomes room to draw. The board now also sits correctly under the mobile browser bar, and exports grow to include anything drawn in the extended area.
