**Shipped v1.229.0** · 2026-10-01

One heart for Favourites, one for the tool. The Workspace top bar showed two identical hearts side by side; now Favourites lives in the header and the open tool's own heart sits in a small toolbar at the right edge. Every heart also stays in sync, keeps one translated name and works by keyboard and touch.

- **Improved — Favourites lives in the header.** The header heart is the one way to Favourites, with its count (also read out by screen readers); the Workspace sidebar and overview no longer repeat it.
- **New — the tool's heart floats at the right edge.** A small toolbar fixed mid-way down the right edge (bottom-right on phones), clear of the tool.
- **Fixed — hearts stay in step.** A pin made in another window or tab shows at once, and a heart never silently undoes a pin it wasn't showing; labels follow a language switch.
- **Fixed — card hearts are proper buttons**, beside the card rather than inside it: separate screen-reader names, pinning keeps your place and focus, visible on touch, no overlap with long names.
- **Fixed — phones.** The Workspace bar grows a second row for a tool's buttons; the site header fits at 360px and in long languages.
- **Fixed — smaller things:** Arabic menus no longer open under the sidebar, collapsed-sidebar icons have names, matching top-bar button sizes, "Favourites" instead of "Pinned", the closed Tools menu is out of the tab order, and the home page shows its tool count again.
