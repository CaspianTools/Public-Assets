**Caspian Office now keeps a public release log — right here in Discussions.**

From now on, every shipped update to [Caspian Office](https://caspianoffice.io) gets its own release-note entry in **Announcements**: what changed, the version, and a link straight to the live tool. No newsletter, no backend — just an open, append-only devlog you can follow, search, and reply to.

Caspian Office is a 100% client-side, offline-first, no-AI office toolkit — PDF, image, text, data, productivity and halal-finance tools that run entirely in your browser (and offline as a single downloadable file).

- 🆕 Announcements will now carry a post per release
- 🔗 Try it: https://caspianoffice.io
- 📝 Full changelog: https://caspianoffice.io/changelog

Watch this category to get pinged on every new tool and improvement.
