**Shipped v1.113.2** · 2026-07-03

The **Updates** page — the built-in version checker — now reads in your language too. That's the whole app frame plus every info page (About, Privacy, Updates) fully localized.

- 🌍 **Improved — Updates page translated:** the status card, the library-versions table (bundled / latest / status labels and the summary line), the settings, relative times ("2 min ago"), and the update/confirm flow all switch with the header globe — in all 12 languages, right-to-left for Arabic.

What's still English, by design: the **Changelog** (an append-only history log) and the insides of each individual tool — the tool-internals translation is the next wave.
