**Shipped v1.116.2** · 2026-07-09

Twelve more tools now read in your language, *inside* — the everyday productivity & time tools, fully translated into all 12 languages, right-to-left for Arabic. Still 100% client-side, no-AI, works offline.

- **Improved — Productivity & time tools localized.** The **calendar event maker**, **countdown**, **habit tracker**, **Pomodoro timer**, **shift scheduler**, **sticky notes**, **stopwatch**, **multi-timer**, **timesheet**, **world clock**, **typing test** and **tally counter** now speak your language throughout — labels, buttons, running status, weekday/month names and the "About this tool" guides — in all 12 languages including Arabic (RTL). Running timers survive a language switch.

That brings the "translated inside" count to **108 tools**.
