**Shipped v1.116.6** · 2026-07-10

The built-in PDF tools start speaking your language *inside* — first eight of the core PDF page tools, fully translated into all 12 languages, right-to-left for Arabic. Still 100% client-side, no-AI, works offline.

- **Improved — Core PDF page tools localized.** **Split**, **Merge**, **Remove pages**, **Extract pages**, **Rotate**, **Organise**, **Add page numbers** and **Unlock** now read in your language throughout — drop-zones, toolbars, options, page labels and status messages — in all 12 languages including Arabic (RTL). Your PDFs never leave your device.

That brings the "translated inside" count to **136 tools** — 16 built-in PDF & image tools to go.
