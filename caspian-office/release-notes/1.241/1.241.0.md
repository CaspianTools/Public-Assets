# Caspian Office 1.241.0: Design and print a one-page year calendar

The new Yearly calendar maker puts a whole year on one printable page. Choose a month grid, rows or a wall planner, add week numbers and day counts, set your own weekend and colour in your holidays, then download a PDF or PNG.

- **New — Three layouts, any paper.** Month grid, rows or wall planner, starting in any month, in A4, A3, Letter, Legal or Tabloid.
- **New — Week numbers, weekends and day counts.** ISO or 1-January week numbers, your own weekend days, the day number and days left, working days per month and optional Hijri dates.
- **New — Special days and day counting.** Colour holidays and terms, add Ramadan and the Eids in one click, and Shift-click two days to count the days between.

Free, private and offline: your calendar is made in your browser and never leaves it.

## Full changes

### Added
- **Yearly calendar maker.** Design a whole year on one printable page in three layouts: a **month grid**, **rows** with the weekdays lined up, or a **wall planner** with a column per month and room to write. Start in any month for a financial or academic year, and download a one-page PDF or PNG in A4, A3, Letter, Legal or Tabloid.
- **Week numbers, weekends and day counts.** ISO 8601 or “week 1 has 1 January” week numbers, any week start, a weekend preset or your own weekend days, the day number and days left on each date, working days per month, and optional Hijri dates.
- **Special days and day counting.** Colour holidays, terms and deadlines (one day or a range, optionally a day off), add Ramadan, the Eids, the Day of Arafah, Ashura and the Islamic New Year in one click, import an .ics file or the all-day events from the Calendar planner, and Shift-click two days to count the days, weeks and working days between them. Export the day list as CSV and the special days as .ics.
