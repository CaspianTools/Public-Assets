# Caspian Office 1.236.0: Cleaner cut-outs, with hair and fur at full size and no colour halo

The Background remover's cut-outs now follow strands of hair and fur at your photo's full size. They no longer leave a fringe of the old background's colour around the edges, and you can add a shadow or save the mask on its own.

- **New — Refine edges.** The outline is re-fitted to the full-size photo, so hair, fur and soft edges come out clean instead of blurry.
- **New — No colour halo.** Edge pixels get the subject's own colour back, so a green or grey fringe no longer shows on a new background.
- **New — Shadows and a mask download.** Add a drop or soft shadow, or save the black-and-white mask for another editor.
- **Fixed — Blocky edges on large photos.** The model's outline is now enlarged smoothly.

Still free, still private: your photo never leaves your browser.

## Full changes

### Added
- **Refine edges.** The Background remover's AI model works on a reduced copy of your photo, so at full size its outline was soft. Refine edges (on by default) re-fits the outline to the full-size photo, so it follows strands of hair, fur and soft outlines. Untick it to see the model's raw cut-out.
- **No colour halo.** Edge pixels used to keep some of the old background's colour, which showed as a green or grey fringe on a new background. Each partly transparent pixel now gets the subject's own colour back.
- **Shadows.** Add a Drop or Soft shadow under the cut-out and set its strength, in both Auto and Plain colour mode.
- **Download the mask.** Next to the transparent PNG you can now save the black-and-white cut-out mask, to use as a layer mask in another editor.

### Fixed
- **Blocky edges on large photos.** The model's outline was enlarged pixel by pixel, which left stair-steps on photos bigger than the model's input. It is now enlarged smoothly.
