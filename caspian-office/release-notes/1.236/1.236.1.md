# Caspian Office 1.236.1: Background remover in one step, plus seven library updates

Add an image and the background is gone. There's nothing to pick or slide first, and a Download PNG button sits right under the result.

- **Fixed — One step.** The background is removed with the AI the moment you add an image. The extra controls wait in a closed More options panel.
- **Fixed — No more stuck settings.** The tool used to remember a mode and slider position that could leave later photos half-removed. It now starts fresh each time.
- **Fixed — No holes in windows.** On shaded backdrops, the colour mode keeps parts of your subject that match the background, such as a car's windscreen.
- **Improved — Seven library updates**, including pdf.js 6.5 and DOMPurify 3.4.17.

Free, private and offline: your photo never leaves your browser.

## Full changes

### Fixed
- **Removing a background took too many steps, and could get stuck in the wrong mode.** The Background remover now removes the background the moment you add an image, always with the AI, and shows a **Download PNG** button under the result. The mode, slider, edge and shadow controls are tucked into a closed **More options** panel. The tool used to remember the mode and slider, so one visit in Plain colour mode left every later photo half-removed; now nothing carries over except the Refine edges setting.
- **No more holes in windows and reflections.** On a shaded or gradient backdrop, Plain colour mode now traces the background in from the image edges instead of removing every pixel of a similar colour, so parts of your subject that match the backdrop, such as a car's windscreen, stay.

### Changed
- **A result even when the AI can't load.** If the AI model can't download, the background is removed by colour instead and a note says why, rather than showing only an error.
- **Seven libraries updated** to their latest releases: pdf.js 6.5.438, mediabunny 1.65.2, DOMPurify 3.4.17, Chart.js 4.6.1, marked 18.1.3, Transformers.js 4.3.2 and qrcode-generator 2.0.4.
