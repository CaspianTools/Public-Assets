# Caspian Office 1.238.0: A full calendar in your browser, with prayer times and clash warnings

The new Calendar planner is a private calendar you plan by dragging. Create, move and resize events on a time grid. Prayer times appear across your week, and a red warning shows whenever two bookings overlap.

- **New — Drag and drop.** Day, Week, Month and Agenda views; drag across the hours to create, drag to move, and drag an edge to resize.
- **New — Prayer times.** Calculated on your device for your city or location, with nine methods and Standard or Hanafi Asr.
- **New — Clash warnings.** Overlapping events, or a meeting over a prayer time, are outlined in red and listed in the sidebar.
- **New — Export and import.** CSV, JSON, .ics or a PDF of the current view, and import from JSON, .ics or CSV.

Free, private and offline: your calendar never leaves your browser.

## Full changes

### Added
- **Calendar planner.** A private calendar with **Day**, **Week**, **Month** and **Agenda** views. Click or drag across the hours to create an event, drag it to another day or time, and drag its bottom edge to change its length. In Month view and the all-day row, drag events between days.
- **Prayer times.** Switch them on in the sidebar, pick a city, type coordinates or use your location, and the five daily prayers appear as bands across Day and Week view, with today's times listed. They are calculated on your device from the sun's position with the method you choose (Umm al-Qura, Muslim World League, ISNA, Egyptian, Karachi, Gulf, Diyanet, Oman or Indonesia & Malaysia) and Standard or Hanafi Asr. **Keep free** sets how long each band lasts.
- **Clash warnings.** Events that overlap each other, or a prayer band, get a red outline and a ⚠ sign. A **Clashes in view** list in the sidebar opens each one, and the event editor names what it clashes with. Turn the warnings off in Settings.
- **Colour categories, repeats and search.** Name and colour your own categories and hide any of them with one click. Events can repeat every day, every weekday, Sunday to Thursday, weekly, fortnightly, monthly or yearly, with an end date, and you can remove a single date from a series. Search finds an event up to a year either side, and a **Time in view** panel shows how your hours split by category.
- **Made for your week.** Start the week on Monday, Saturday or Sunday. Choose the hours shown, a 12- or 24-hour clock and 15, 30 or 60-minute snapping, and turn on **Hijri dates** (Umm al-Qura) and week numbers.
- **Export and import.** Download a **CSV** (Google Calendar's column names), a **JSON** backup, an **.ics** file with repeats, or a **PDF** of the current view. Import a JSON backup, an .ics file or a CSV, replacing your events or adding to them. Undo and redo cover every change, and everything stays on your device.
