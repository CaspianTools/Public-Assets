# Caspian Office 1.239.1: Choose your own weekend in the Calendar planner

The weekend is now its own setting in the Calendar planner, so a Friday–Saturday weekend works with any week start. In the Gulf and other Friday–Saturday countries, a new calendar now starts on Sunday with Friday and Saturday as the weekend.

- **Improved — Set the weekend.** Saturday and Sunday, Friday and Saturday, Thursday and Friday, Friday only or Sunday only.
- **Improved — Your local week from the start.** New calendars follow your time zone; ones you already use keep their week.

Free, private and offline: your calendar never leaves your browser.

🔗 Live: https://caspianoffice.io/tool/calendar-planner/
📝 Changelog: https://caspianoffice.io/changelog

---

## Full changes

### Changed
- **Set the weekend.** The Calendar planner has a **Weekend** setting, separate from the week start: Saturday and Sunday, Friday and Saturday, Thursday and Friday, Friday only or Sunday only. Weekend days are shaded in every view, and "show weekends" hides exactly those days.
- **Your local week on the first visit.** In the Gulf, Egypt, Jordan, Iraq, Bangladesh and other countries with a Friday–Saturday weekend, a new calendar now starts on Sunday with Friday and Saturday as the weekend. Calendars you have already set up keep their week as it was.
