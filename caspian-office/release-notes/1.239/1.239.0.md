# Caspian Office 1.239.0: A printable year planner, a 2-week view and drag to copy

The Calendar planner now shows your whole year at a glance and prints it on one page. You can plan two weeks side by side and copy an event just by dragging it.

- **New — Year planner.** Twelve months at once, with busy days tinted in their category colour. Download it as a one-page PDF.
- **New — 2-week view.** Fourteen days on the time grid, two weeks per click.
- **New — Drag to copy.** Hold Alt (or Ctrl / ⌘) while dragging to drop a copy instead of moving the event.
- **Improved — Hours in the toolbar.** Show only your working day, such as 06:00 to 18:00, in one click.

Free, private and offline: your calendar never leaves your browser.

## Full changes

### Added
- **Year planner.** The Calendar planner has a **Year** view: all twelve months at once, each busy day tinted in its category colour, with dots when several categories share a day. Click a month or a day to open it. The PDF puts the whole year on one landscape page with a category legend, ready to print.
- **2-week view.** See fourteen days side by side on the time grid; the arrows move two weeks at a time.
- **Drag to copy.** Hold **Alt** (or Ctrl / ⌘) while dragging an event to drop a copy instead of moving it, on the time grid, in Month view and in the all-day row. A copy of a repeating event is a one-off on the day you drop it.

### Changed
- **Hours in the toolbar.** Choose the first and last hour of your day, for example 06:00 to 18:00, straight from the toolbar in Day, Week and 2-week views instead of opening Settings.
