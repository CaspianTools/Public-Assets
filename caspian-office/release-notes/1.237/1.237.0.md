# Caspian Office 1.237.0: Updates move into the Changelog; the background remover waits for your click

The Changelog page now also checks for updates, so the separate Updates page is gone. The background remover does nothing until you click. Images on a plain background are cut out instantly, with nothing to download.

- **Improved — One page for what's new.** The update check and its setting sit at the top of the Changelog.
- **Fixed — No loading until you click.** The background remover no longer starts the AI as soon as you choose an image.
- **New — Plain backgrounds are instant.** A product on black or white is cut out straight away, and dark details such as tyres stay.
- **Fixed — No endless loading.** If the AI stops responding, the background is removed by colour instead, with a note saying why.

Free, private and offline: your files never leave your browser.

## Full changes

### Added
- **Plain backgrounds are instant.** When the whole edge of the image is one colour (a product on black or white, for example), the background is removed straight away with no download. Dark details such as tyres stay.

### Changed
- **One page for what's new.** The update check now sits at the top of the Changelog page, so the version you're running, the **Check now** button and the check-on-launch setting are right above the release history. The separate Updates page is gone; old links, the bell and the menu all open the Changelog.
- **No public library list.** The table of bundled library versions is no longer shown.

### Fixed
- **The Background remover started loading the AI as soon as you chose an image, and could sit at "Loading model… 0%".** Now nothing happens until you click **Remove background**.
- **No more endless loading.** If the AI model stops responding for 30 seconds, the background is removed by colour instead and a note says why.
