# Caspian Office 1.230.1: Background remover's Auto mode works again

Auto (AI) background removal failed on every image after the model finished loading, with a misleading "check your connection" message. It now cuts out your subject again.

- **Fixed — Auto (AI) mode no longer stops with "mask size mismatch".** The cut-out mask the model returns is now read correctly.
- **Fixed — Masks of a different size are resized to fit.** If the mask ever comes back at a different size from your image, it is resized to match instead of failing.

Background removal that runs in your browser, so your image never leaves your device.

🔗 Live: https://caspianoffice.io/tool/bg-remover/
📝 Changelog: https://caspianoffice.io/changelog

## Full changes

### Fixed
- **Auto (AI) background removal failed on every image.** After the model finished loading, Remove background stopped with "Check your connection and retry (mask size mismatch)", even with a working connection. The cut-out mask the model returns is now read correctly, and it is resized to fit if its size ever differs from your image.
