# Caspian Office 1.242.1: The Calendar planner now fills your screen

On desktop, the Calendar planner's day and week timeline now runs all the way to the bottom of your window, so you see more hours at once and no space is wasted below.

- **Improved — Full-height timeline.** Day, Week and 2-week views reach the bottom of the screen and follow the window as you resize it, on the site and in the Workspace.

Free, private and offline: your calendar never leaves your browser.

🔗 Live: https://caspianoffice.io/tool/calendar-planner/
📝 Changelog: https://caspianoffice.io/changelog

---

## Full changes

### Changed
- **Full-height timeline.** On desktop, the Day, Week and 2-week grid of the Calendar planner now runs down to the bottom of your screen instead of stopping short and leaving empty space below. It follows the window as you resize it, on the site and in the Workspace.
