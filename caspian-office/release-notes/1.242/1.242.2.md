# Caspian Office 1.242.2: The Calendar planner's month view now fills your screen

On desktop, the month view's weeks now stretch to the bottom of your window, so each day has room to show more of its events at a glance.

- **Improved — Full-height month.** The weeks share the whole screen height, and each day lists as many events as fit before "+N more". It follows the window as you resize it.

Free, private and offline: your calendar never leaves your browser.

🔗 Live: https://caspianoffice.io/tool/calendar-planner/
📝 Changelog: https://caspianoffice.io/changelog

## Full changes

### Changed
- **Full-height month.** On desktop, the Month view of the Calendar planner now stretches its weeks down to the bottom of your screen, and each day shows as many events as its taller box can hold before "+N more". It follows the window as you resize it.
