# Caspian Office 1.242.0: A scrolling timeline, reminders and Islamic dates in the Calendar planner

The Calendar planner's day and week views are now one timeline you scroll through, and events can carry reminders, flexible repeats and run past midnight. Islamic key dates and a full Print / PDF dialog round it off.

- **New — Scroll through your days.** Day, Week and 2-week views form one strip you scroll sideways, with the hours and dates pinned, plus Now and zoom.
- **New — Reminders and custom repeats.** Get a reminder before an event, and repeat it every N weeks, on chosen days, on the 3rd Thursday or on the same Hijri date.
- **New — Islamic dates and templates.** Ramadan, the Eids, Arafah and the white days are marked; save any event as a reusable template.
- **New — Print / PDF.** A page per day, week or month over any dates, on A4, A3, Letter or Legal.

Free, private and offline: your calendar never leaves your browser.

## Full changes

### Added
- **A timeline you scroll through.** Day, Week and 2-week view are now one continuous strip of days: scroll sideways with a trackpad, Shift + wheel or a swipe, with the hours and day headers pinned. ‹ › glide one period at a time and settle on a day edge, **Now** brings the current time into view, and every visit opens on the present.
- **Zoom the hours** with a slider, Ctrl + scroll, a pinch, or the + and − keys.
- **Reminders.** Up to five per event, from “at the start” to a week before. They appear on the page — in whichever tool you have open — and as a system notification when the window is in the background, with Snooze 5 min and Open. Exported to .ics as alarms.
- **Custom repeats.** Every N days, weeks, months or years; chosen weekdays; the 3rd Thursday or last Friday of the month; the same Hijri date, such as every 1 Ramadan; and series that end after a number of times. A summary under the editor reads the rule back.
- **Events past midnight.** A timed event can end on a later day — a night shift or a trip — and is drawn across each day it covers, with clashes, totals and exports counting it correctly.
- **Templates.** Save an event as a template from its editor, then click its chip to add it at the next free time or drag it straight onto the grid. The editor also offers **From template**.
- **Islamic dates.** The start of Ramadan, the two Eids, the Day of Arafah, the Islamic New Year and Ashura are marked, the white days (13th–15th) shaded and each Hijri month’s first day outlined, with a “coming up” list and a one-day shift to match local moon sighting.
- **Print / PDF…** A day, week, two weeks or month per page, a year page or an agenda, over any dates, on A4, A3, Letter or Legal, portrait or landscape, with a category legend, Hijri dates, prayer times and an ink-saving black-and-white option. The PDF button still downloads the view on screen in one click.

### Changed
- **Undo at hand.** Moving, copying or deleting an event shows an **Undo** toast, and **Delete all events** downloads a backup file first unless you untick it. N now jumps to the current time and C adds an event.
