You can now download everything you have in the Workspace as a single file.
Go to Profile → Your data and click Download my data to get your profile, your workspaces, and the tasks, notes, flows, checklists, sprints, milestones and projects you can access, plus the comments you wrote.
Your GitHub connection and AI key are never included in the file.
