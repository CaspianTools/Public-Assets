You can now permanently delete your account yourself from Profile → Your data.
Before anything is deleted, you see which of your workspaces will go and which ones you will leave, and you confirm by typing your email and signing in again.
Workspaces you own on your own are deleted with everything in them. In workspaces shared with others, the tasks and notes you created stay for the team, but your comments, messages and profile are removed.
If you own a workspace that other people still use, or one with an active subscription, you will be asked to sort that out first so nobody loses their work.
