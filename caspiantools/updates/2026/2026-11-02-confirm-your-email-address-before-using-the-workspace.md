If you sign up with an email address and a password, you now get a confirmation link in your inbox, and the Workspace waits until you have clicked it.
If you already have a password account that was never confirmed, you will see the same page on your next visit, with a button to send the link again.
Signing in with Google or GitHub needs no extra step.
Invitations to a workspace can only be accepted from a confirmed address, so nobody can join your team by guessing an invited email.
