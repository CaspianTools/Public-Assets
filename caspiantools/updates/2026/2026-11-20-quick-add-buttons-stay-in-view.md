In the New item dialog, the Cancel and Create buttons now stay pinned to the bottom of the window while you scroll through a long form. Before, they scrolled away with the fields, so on a smaller screen you had to scroll down to find them. This applies to new tasks, notes, checklists, flows, sprints, milestones and projects.
