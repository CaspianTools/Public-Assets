The flow editor now works like a dedicated diagram tool. Lines can be curved, straight or right-angled, with arrows at either end, and you can drag a line's end onto another shape. There are five new shapes (database, document, note, person and group box), text size, bold, alignment and colour, plus align, spread out and front or back ordering.
Tidy up arranges a whole diagram in one click, the + button starts from one of six templates or imports Mermaid code, and Export can save or copy Mermaid code.
Any shape can link to a task, showing its number and status, and anyone on the flow can leave comments on a shape and resolve them.
When teammates open the same flow you see their cursors and what they have selected.
