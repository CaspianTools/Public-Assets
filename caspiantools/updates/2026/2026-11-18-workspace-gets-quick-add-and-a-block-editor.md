Workspace has a calmer layout: the page title sits under the top bar, filters slide in from the right and show as chips under the toolbar, and every table heading sorts.
The green New button opens Quick add, one list of everything you can create, from a task to a project; pick one and fill in its form.
Task descriptions now use the same block editor as notes, so you can add headings, lists and checklists without writing markdown.
Save and bulk-action bars float at the bottom of the screen, so they stay in reach on long pages.
