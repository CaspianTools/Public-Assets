The purple fish you know from the browser tab now appears across the site and the Workspace: the sign-in page, the site header and footer, the mobile menu, and the sidebar in the app.
Until now those places showed a generic placeholder, and the Workspace tab icon was a two-letter monogram; everything uses the same mark.
It follows your light or dark theme, so it stays readable either way.
