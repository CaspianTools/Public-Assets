title: "VS Code pairings are now limited to one workspace and 180 days"
date: 2026-11-02
type: notice
social: false
draft: false
---

When you connect the Caspian Taskmaster or Caspian Notes extension, the connection now opens only the workspace you picked, and only your tasks and notes there.
It also expires 180 days after you paired; the extension then shows a Reconnect button and you pair again in the browser.
Connections made before today keep working until 1 February 2027.
Update to Caspian Taskmaster 1.31.4 or Caspian Notes 1.4.9 to get the Reconnect prompt; older versions keep working but only report the problem in their log.
