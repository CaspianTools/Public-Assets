title: "Deleted tasks now go to Trash, and you can restore them"
date: 2026-10-01
type: feature
social: true
draft: false
---

Deleting a task in the Workspace no longer erases it. It moves to the new Trash, under Tasks in the sidebar, where anyone in the workspace can restore it with its comments and attachments for 30 days.
After 30 days a trashed task is removed for good, and only workspace owners and admins can delete one forever sooner.
If the task was linked to a GitHub issue, that issue is closed while the task is in Trash and reopened when you restore it.
