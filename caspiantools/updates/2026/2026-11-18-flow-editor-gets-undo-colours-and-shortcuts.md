You can now undo and redo changes in a flow with the arrow buttons next to the shapes, or with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z. Select any shapes or lines and a bar appears at the bottom of the canvas: pick one of nine colours, make a line dashed, duplicate or delete. Shapes can be resized from their corners, and copy, paste, select all, arrow-key nudging and snap to grid all work as you would expect. The keyboard button in the top-right corner of the canvas lists every shortcut.
