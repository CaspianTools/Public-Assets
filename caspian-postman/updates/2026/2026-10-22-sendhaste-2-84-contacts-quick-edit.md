Click a contact in Contacts and a panel opens beside the list, where you can fill in their name, company, job title, phone, relationship and tags and press Save, without leaving the list. Open full page shows the rest.
The list is easier to read: Seen by is now one menu instead of a row of buttons, empty cells stay blank, and the row buttons show when you point at a row.
Tick contacts and the actions show with their names: Email, Relationship, Tag, Delete. Click a column title to sort, and again to reverse the order.
The sidebar shows how many contacts each list holds, and Needs a look, above the list, points to contacts without a name and addresses that bounced.
Step by step: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/contacts
