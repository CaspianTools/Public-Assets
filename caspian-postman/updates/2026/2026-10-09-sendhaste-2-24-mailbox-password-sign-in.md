When an administrator creates a mailbox, the password shown once now also signs in to SendHaste. On the sign-in page, type the mailbox's address and that password, and click **Sign in**. There's no confirmation email to wait for, and the person sees only that mailbox. If the password is ever shared by mistake, **New password** on the mailbox stops the old one at once and signs that address out everywhere. Step by step: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/add-person
