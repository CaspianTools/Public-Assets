You can now reach the SendHaste team without leaving the app. Open the account menu (your picture, top right) and choose Support: write a subject and a message, click Send ticket, and follow our answer right there. Reply to keep the conversation going, and close the ticket when it's solved. Org Super Admins see every ticket from their organization. The help center is linked from Support too: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/support.
