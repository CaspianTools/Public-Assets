You can now use your templates when writing from any of your mailboxes, drafts included. In the email form, pick one under Design: it opens in the email so you can change the text, buttons and pictures before you send. Templates → Use in an email opens it in the right mailbox too. How templates work: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/templates-groups.
