New organizations now get a Get started checklist that walks them from adding their domain to reading its mail in SendHaste, with a reminder on the Inbox until setup is finished. Each domain shows exactly which DNS records to add where you bought it (MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC), with copy buttons and a Check records button that tells you when they're in place. You'll find it all under Settings → Get started and Settings → Domains.
