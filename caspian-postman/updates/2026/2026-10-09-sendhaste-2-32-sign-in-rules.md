Organizations can now set their own access policy. In **Settings → Organization → Sign-in**, an admin chooses the **Shortest password** allowed, how often everyone has to **Sign in again** (every day, every 3 days or every 5 days), and **Allowed networks**, so SendHaste can only be used from your offices. Anyone whose password is too short is asked for a new one the next time they sign in. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/sign-in-security
