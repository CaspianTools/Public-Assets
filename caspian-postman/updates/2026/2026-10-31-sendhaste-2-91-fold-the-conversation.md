The email you open is now a foldable line of its conversation like all the others, so you can unfold the newest reply and fold the one you came from, and read only what you want.
A button at the right of "emails in this conversation" folds every email at once, or unfolds them all.
Every line of a conversation now shows the time as well as the date, and on a phone an email's date and time stay inside the card.
More in the help center: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/inbox
