Making something new now always works the same way. The **+** in the header (**Quick add**) holds every form for creating something, and the create buttons on the pages, such as **Add a user**, **Create a mailbox**, **New rule** or **Make an API key**, open the same popup. Quick add also offers new shortcuts: **Users from a file**, **Role**, **Organization rule**, **Team address**, and the labels and rules of the mailbox you have open. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/getting-started
