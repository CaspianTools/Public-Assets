Org Super Admins have new tools in **Settings**. Under **Organization** → **Sign-in**, **Sign out when idle for** signs out a browser nobody has used for 30 minutes up to 8 hours. On a person's page under **Users**, **Block** keeps someone out while keeping their roles and mailboxes, and **Sending** sets a lower daily limit or keeps them to emailing colleagues only. **Add many from a file** adds a whole team from a spreadsheet. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/people-access
