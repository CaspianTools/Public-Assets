Everyone can now make their own labels. Under **Labels** in the mail sidebar, **Edit labels** adds, renames, recolours and deletes labels for the mailbox you're reading, and everyone who reads that mailbox sees them. Your organization's labels stay, and you can hide one or give it your own name and colour, just for you. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/labels-rules
