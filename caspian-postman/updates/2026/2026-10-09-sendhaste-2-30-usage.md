Admins can now see at a glance how the organization's mailboxes are used. Open **Settings → Usage** to find every mailbox with its storage, plus how many emails it sent and received each day over the last 30 days, drawn as a small chart. Need the numbers elsewhere? **Download as CSV** gives them day by day. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/people-access
