If you sign in to SendHaste with your email address and a password, you can now also connect a Google account, such as your Gmail. Open **Settings** → **Sign-in and security** → **Google accounts** and click **Connect a Google account**: from then on, **Continue with Google** with that account signs you in to your own address, and your password keeps working. Org Super Admins can connect one for a person under **Users** → **Sign-in**, and Domain Super Admins can for the people on their domains. You get an email each time a Google account is connected, and **Recent sign-ins** shows which one was used. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/sign-in-security
