Org Super Admins can now choose which notifications people in their organization get under the bell. Go to **Settings** → **Organization** → **Notifications**, where **Organization access**, **Domain access** and **Support answers** each have their own switch, then click **Save**. Switching a type off stops new notifications of that kind; ones already sent stay. More in the help center: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/notifications
