Giving a new colleague their own mailbox is quicker. After **Add user** in Settings → Users, the page shows the next step: give them a mailbox on one of your domains. When you create a mailbox, the new **Assign to** field gives it to them right away. The full address shows as you type, and an address that already exists is caught before you save.

A new mailbox's password now stays hidden until you click **Show password**, and **Done** waits until you confirm you've copied it. The page also tells you who can read the new mailbox.

Smaller improvements: signatures now show the address you send from, a message after saving no longer comes back when you reload the page, errors appear where you're looking, and the domain list no longer counts the catch-all as an extra email.

The help center has a new page with the whole process as a diagram, from adding someone to telling them how to sign in: [Add a person with their own mailbox](https://sendhaste.com/en/help/add-person).
