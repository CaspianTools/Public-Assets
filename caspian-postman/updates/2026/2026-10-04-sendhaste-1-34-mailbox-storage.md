Each mailbox on your own domain comes with 5 GB of storage, and you can now see how much of it is used under Settings → Domains → your domain. From 90 % a mailbox is marked "Nearly full", and its users see a notice at the top of their mail, so there is time to clear out old mail with large attachments before new mail gets held back.
