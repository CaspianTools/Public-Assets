Deleted an email forever by mistake? You now have 30 days to bring it back. Open **Deleted Items**, click **Recover deleted items**, tick the emails you want and click **Restore selected**: they go back to Deleted Items, and drafts to Drafts. Everyone who reads the mailbox can do this, so one wrong click no longer loses a client's email. Step by step: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/inbox
