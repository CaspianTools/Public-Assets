An email and its replies now show as one row in the mail list, with the number of emails beside the sender, and an open email lists the rest of its conversation at the top. Archiving, flagging or deleting a conversation row does it to the whole conversation. Prefer one row per email? Switch off **Conversation view** in **Settings** → **Reading pane**. **Settings** → **Display** also has a **Text size** and a **Compact** mail list. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/inbox
