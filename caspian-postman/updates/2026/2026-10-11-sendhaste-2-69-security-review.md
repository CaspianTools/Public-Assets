SendHaste had a security review of the whole app, and this version carries its improvements: every page now tells your browser exactly which scripts may run and who may show it inside another page, requests another website makes on your behalf are refused, and your browser keeps no copy of your mail after you sign out.

Your sign-in is yours alone. An organization's admins can connect a Google account, reset two-step sign-in, make a new mailbox password or sign someone out everywhere only for people who aren't also in another organization or on the SendHaste team. Recovery codes are shown on the page itself, no longer in the page's address.

Two smaller changes: Not spam unblocks a sender for your domain, while a sender an Org Super Admin blocked for the whole organization stays blocked unless an Org Super Admin lifts it; and Add many from a file gives new people only the mailboxes listed on the domains you manage. Read more in Help: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/sign-in-security
