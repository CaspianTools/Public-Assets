Your website's automatic emails, like a welcome message, a password reset or an order confirmation, can now use templates you design in SendHaste. In Settings → API keys, under Emails your websites send, connect a name such as welcome to one of your templates. Your website then sends that name with the person's details, which go into the template wherever you wrote {{params.first_name}}. To change an email later, just pick another template for its name; nothing changes on the website. Send a test lets you try it on your own address first, and the guide includes ready-to-copy examples for developers: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/api.
