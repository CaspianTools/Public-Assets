Fake invoices and harmful attachments now stop before they reach anyone's Inbox. Emails that pretend to come from your own domains, come from a look-alike of them (such as yourcompany.co), or carry a program or an Office file with macros are held for review, and the Inbox says how many were held. Your organization's admins are notified and decide under **Settings → Held emails**: **Release** lets an email through, **Delete** removes it. It's on for everyone; admins can switch it off in **Settings → Organization → Spam filter**. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/spam
