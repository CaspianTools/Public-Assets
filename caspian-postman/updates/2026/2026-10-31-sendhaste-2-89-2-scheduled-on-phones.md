On a phone, the Scheduled page now shows each waiting email on its own lines: who it goes to and when it leaves on the first line, the subject under them, and the Cancel sending button below, instead of the button sitting on top of the name.
Each row starts with "To", so it is clear who the email is for.
After you schedule an email, the message simply says Scheduled. when you land on the Scheduled page.
A link you add in the message editor now looks like a link while you write: coloured and underlined.
More in the help center: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
