The **Mailbox** button in the header now keeps the mailbox you were reading when you go to Contacts, Templates or Settings. Clicking **Mail** takes you back to that same mailbox instead of switching to another one. The first time, Mail opens the mailbox at the top of the list. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/inbox
