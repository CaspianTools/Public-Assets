Pressing Enter in To, Cc or Bcc now just ends the address, and an email with no subject or no message asks before it goes, with Send anyway and Cancel.
Compose always starts from a mailbox that can send, and picking another one in From brings that mailbox's own signature.
Saved blocks from Insert go where your cursor is, above your signature, with or without formatting and in templates, in the template's own font.
A template now starts without your signature, since it has its own sign-off; pick one and you see it at the end, so an email is never signed twice.
Sent Items shows whole subjects, and the icon buttons have names that screen readers say. More in the help center: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
