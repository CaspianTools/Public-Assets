You can now connect your own domain to SendHaste and create its mailboxes yourself, without waiting for our team. Under Settings → Domains, open your domain and click Connect: SendHaste lists exactly which DNS records to add where you bought the domain, required ones first, and checks them for you with one click. Once the records are found, the domain switches on by itself and you can create mailboxes like info@ or sales@ right there; each new mailbox shows its password once, for mail apps on phones and computers. New domains start with a lower daily sending limit for their first three weeks, shown on the domain's page, to build a good reputation with other mail providers. The sign-in page also got smaller touches: your logo at full size, a compact language menu, and the logo now takes you to the home page.
