You can now **Flag** an email to come back to it, from the list or at the top of an open email, and flags show in Outlook and phone mail apps too. Under the folders in the mail sidebar, **Flagged**, **Unread** and **All mail** list those emails from the whole mailbox. For a narrower search, click **Search options** above the list: search by **From**, **To**, **Subject**, words, dates, folder or label, or only emails with attachments, unread or flagged. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/inbox
