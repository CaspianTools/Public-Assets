Every email from your team can now carry the same, on-brand signature. An organization's admin opens **Settings → Domains**, picks the domain and goes to **Company signature**: set the style, colour, company name, website, address, a logo and a **Legal footer**, and see a preview straight away. Turn on **Lock it** and people can't change those details; they fill in only their own name, title and phone numbers in their profile. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
