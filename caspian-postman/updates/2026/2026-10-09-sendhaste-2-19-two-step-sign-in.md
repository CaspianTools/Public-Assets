You can now protect your SendHaste account with a code from an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator, after your password. Open **Settings → Sign-in and security** and click **Turn on**. You get ten recovery codes in case you lose your phone. The same page lists your recent sign-ins, we email you when you sign in from a new device, and **Sign out everywhere** signs you out on every computer and phone at once. Administrators can require two-step sign-in for password sign-ins, or allow Google sign-in only, under **Settings → Organization → Sign-in**. Step by step: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/sign-in-security
