The **+** button in the header (**Quick add**) now opens more forms right in the popup. Everyone can send a **Support request** from it. Org Super Admins can also add an **Email label**, a **User**, a **Domain**, an **Email address** or an **API key** without leaving the page they are on, and Domain Super Admins can add email addresses on their own domains. Each person sees only what their role lets them add. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/getting-started
