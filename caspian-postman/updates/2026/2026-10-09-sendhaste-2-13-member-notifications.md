People without an admin role now see a shorter **Settings → Notifications**: only the notifications that can reach them, in three groups (**About you**, **Your mailboxes** and **Support**), worded for them. Notifications meant for administrators, like a domain being switched on or API keys, are no longer listed there.

**Domain access changed** and **Mailbox access removed** are always on for them, so everyone is told when their access to mail changes. Administrators keep the full list. More in [Notifications](https://sendhaste.com/en/help/notifications).
