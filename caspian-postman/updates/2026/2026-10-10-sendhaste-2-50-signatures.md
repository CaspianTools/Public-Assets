In **Your profile** → **Signature** you can now keep more signatures of your own, such as a short one for replies. Choose what new emails start with and what replies and forwards start with, and set a signature for each address you send from. In Compose, the **Signature** list next to Attach swaps the signature in your message. A company signature locked by your administrator still applies to its domain. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
