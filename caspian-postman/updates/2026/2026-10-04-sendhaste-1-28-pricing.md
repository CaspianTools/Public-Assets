SendHaste now has a pricing page: one plan at $1 per user per month, with every feature included. Shared addresses like info@ and sales@ don't count as users, so you only pay for the people who sign in. A calculator on the page shows your team's monthly total, and the whole website now wears the SendHaste blue.
