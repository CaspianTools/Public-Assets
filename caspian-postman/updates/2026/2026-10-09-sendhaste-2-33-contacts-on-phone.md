Your SendHaste contacts can now show up in your phone's contacts and mail apps, and stay up to date. Open **Settings → Sign-in and security → Contacts on your phone**, click **Make a sync password**, then add a CardDAV account on your iPhone, iPad, Mac or Android phone with the details shown. You get the contacts you see in SendHaste. It's read-only for now: change contacts in SendHaste. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/contacts
