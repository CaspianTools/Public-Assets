Everyone can now bring their address book into SendHaste. In **Contacts**, click **Upload contacts** and choose the CSV or vCard (.vcf) file your old mail service or phone exported. Under **Who sees them**, keep **Only me**, or share them with the people who use one of your mailboxes. Uploaded contacts are never shared with the rest of your organization, and contacts imported into a mailbox are now seen only by the people who use that mailbox. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/contacts
