Pages open faster: the mailbox list and an open email do much less waiting before they show, and every page now travels at a fraction of its size.
Search as you type: the first emails that match appear under the search box with their folder and date, and the full results mark the words you searched for.
A mailbox now warns you before it reaches the number of people it may email in a day, at the top of the mailbox and in the writing window, and says what to do past the limit.
**Contacts** come 50 at a time so a long address book opens quickly, every date shows in your own time zone, and empty fields show their samples as "e.g. …".
Also fixed: typing in the search box while a page is still loading no longer opens **New email**, and the unread number in the browser tab is always the open mailbox's Inbox.
More in the help center: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/inbox
