Write now, send when it suits your reader. In a new email, click **Send later**, pick the day and time (in your own time zone, up to 30 days ahead) and click **Schedule**. Until it goes, the email waits under **Scheduled** in the mailbox's sidebar, and **Cancel sending** puts it back in your drafts if plans change. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
