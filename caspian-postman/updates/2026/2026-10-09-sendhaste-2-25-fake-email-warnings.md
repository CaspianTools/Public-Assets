Fake invoices and "urgent payment" requests often look real. When you open an email, SendHaste now warns you in red when its sender couldn't be verified, when it comes from a look-alike of one of your own domains (such as yourcompany.co instead of yourcompany.com), or when someone outside your organization uses a colleague's name. Emails from outside carry an **External** tag, and the first email from someone new says so. When you see a warning, check with the sender another way before paying or sending files. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/spam
