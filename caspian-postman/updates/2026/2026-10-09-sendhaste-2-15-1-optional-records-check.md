On your domain's page in **Settings → Domains**, **Check records** now also looks for the TLS reporting record. Once both optional records are in place, step 3 **Optional (advanced)** shows **Done**, just like steps 1 and 2. Each optional record it finds is marked **Found**. More in [Connect your domain](https://sendhaste.com/en/help/connect-domain).
