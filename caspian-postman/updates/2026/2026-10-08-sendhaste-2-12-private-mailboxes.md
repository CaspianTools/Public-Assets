A new mailbox is now private by default: only the person you pick in **Assign to** reads it. If you want the domain's catch-all holder to see it too, turn on **Also give it to whoever has the catch-all** when you create it. Mailboxes you already have keep the same access, and you can change who reads each one under the domain's **Roles**.

Every help center guide now also opens right inside SendHaste: open **Support** in the account menu and pick one under **Guides**. Administrators find [Add a person with their own mailbox](https://sendhaste.com/en/help/add-person) first, with its step-by-step diagram.
