In Templates, the domain filter is now a dropdown next to List and Cards: type to find a domain, tick as many as you like, and click Apply. Sharing is simpler too: choose Share in a template's ⋮ menu and pick who may use it in one panel. The title, the filter and the view switch now stay at the top while you scroll a long list. Read more in Help, under Templates and group emails: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/templates-groups
