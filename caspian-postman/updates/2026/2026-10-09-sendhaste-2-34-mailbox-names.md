When you create a mailbox, the **Display name** you type is now saved right away: its mail goes out with that name, and you no longer have to set it again under **Sends as**. Making a new password for a mailbox now keeps its sender name and its other addresses. The **Emails** section on a domain's page has a cleaner look too: each mailbox is a card with its settings lined up underneath, and **Create a mailbox** has its own panel. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/connect-domain
