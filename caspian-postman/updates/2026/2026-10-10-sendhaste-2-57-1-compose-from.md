Two small improvements. In Compose, picking another mailbox under **From** now keeps the recipients, subject and message you've already typed; only attachments need adding again. Out-of-office replies now come from the mailbox's name, the one set in **Sends as**, instead of just its address. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/labels-rules
