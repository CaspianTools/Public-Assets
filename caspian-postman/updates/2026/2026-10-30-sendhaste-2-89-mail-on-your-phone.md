Writing an email on a phone now fits the screen: a reply wraps its text, subject and addresses instead of running off the edge, and the formatting bar scrolls inside its row.
To, Cc, Bcc and Reply-to show each address as a chip with the person's name: a comma, Enter or leaving the line ends an address, tapping a chip edits it, and its × removes it.
On a phone, Label, Insert and Send later open as a sheet from the bottom of the screen, and selecting text in the message shows a small formatting strip under it; your phone's own Copy and Paste menu stays as it is.
After Send, the Sending in 5s… message with Undo now sits just above the Send row instead of over it.
More in the help center: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
