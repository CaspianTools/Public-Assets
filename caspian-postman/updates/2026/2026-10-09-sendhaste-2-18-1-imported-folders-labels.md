When you bring mail over with **Import into a mailbox**, the folders you made yourself in your old service (such as Clients) now become labels with the same name, and their emails go to the Archive. Click the label under **Labels** in the mailbox's sidebar to see them. Inbox, Sent, Spam and the like still go to the matching folders, and running an import again doesn't create a label twice. Step by step: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/move-mail
