Connecting your domain to SendHaste is now entirely in your hands. Open Settings → Domains → your domain and click Connect. You get its DNS records right there, and your mail starts arriving once you change your MX record. The Get started checklist now ends with that step instead of waiting for our team. The domain page also no longer shows a "Where its mail lives" section: every domain you connect runs on SendHaste's own mail server. The step-by-step guide is at https://sendhaste.com/en/help/connect-domain.
