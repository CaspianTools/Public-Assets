Two new notifications arrive under the bell. **Domain is active** tells Org Super Admins and the domain's Domain Super Admins when a domain's DNS records check out and it's switched on. **Mailbox almost full** tells the people who have a mailbox when it has used 90% of its storage, so there's time to clean up before mail stops arriving. Both can be switched off in **Settings** → **Notifications**. More in the help center: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/notifications
