In a mailbox's **Rules**, **Allowed senders** lists the people and companies whose mail must never land in Junk Mail. Their mail already there comes back to the Inbox. **Always show pictures from** shows pictures in their emails straight away, and you can add a sender with one click under any of their emails. Org Super Admins can keep both lists for the whole organization, and **Settings** → **Usage** now has a **Spam, last 30 days** report. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/spam
