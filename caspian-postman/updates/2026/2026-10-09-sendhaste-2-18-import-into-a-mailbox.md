Moving to SendHaste from Zoho Mail, Gmail or another service no longer means leaving your old mail behind. An organization's administrator can open **Settings → Import & export → Import into a mailbox**, sign in to the old mailbox, and copy its email straight into SendHaste with a progress bar: folders keep their names and read emails stay read. Exported **.eml** files, or the **.zip** file Zoho Mail exports, can be uploaded too, and so can contacts as a CSV or vCard file. Emails already copied are skipped, so an import can simply be run again. Step by step: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/move-mail
