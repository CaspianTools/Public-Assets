Templates now opens as a list, with a small preview of each template, so a long collection is quicker to scan. Prefer big previews? Switch to Cards at the top right, and the page stays the way you left it. You can also mark which of your domains each template is for, right in the list, then click a domain above the list to see only its templates. Read more in Help, under Templates and group emails: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/templates-groups
