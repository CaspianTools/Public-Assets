Bringing your mail over from another service is more reliable: email files no longer fail partway through an import with a mail server error. Emails up to 50 MB can now be imported, from files and straight from your old service (the limit was 25 MB). A mailbox takes in only so much imported mail each hour; when a large import reaches that, the page now shows when it will carry on and continues by itself, as long as you keep it open. Sending an email with more than four attachments works again too. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/move-mail
