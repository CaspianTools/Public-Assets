The sending API can now tell your apps and websites which of your addresses an API key may send from. Instead of writing a From address into their code, your apps can offer people a choice, and they only ever see addresses the key is allowed to use. Developers will find how it works in Help, under Sending API: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/api
