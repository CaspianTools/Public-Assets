The help center now explains when to tighten your domain's DMARC record. Your record starts with p=none, so no email you send is lost while you set up. After a few weeks, once every service that sends as your domain is set up (your website's forms, an invoicing tool), change it to p=quarantine. Fake emails pretending to be you then go to spam instead of reaching people's Inboxes.

Read more under "After you connect" in [Connect your domain](https://sendhaste.com/en/help/connect-domain).
