All your mail now lives in real mailboxes on your domain. Mail opens your first mailbox, and the Mailbox button in the header switches between them; each has its own Inbox, Drafts, Sent Items, Archive, Junk Mail and Deleted Items. Tick emails and choose Label to sort them with your organization's labels, click a label in the sidebar to see its emails, and search a whole mailbox from the search box. Compose in a mailbox now has your signature, your saved blocks and Undo send. The older shared Inbox views (Starred, Assigned to me, Snoozed, Done, Scheduled) and Inbox rules are gone, and group emails are now switched on for each organization on request from the Support page. Unsubscribe links in group emails sent before today no longer work. How it works: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/inbox.
