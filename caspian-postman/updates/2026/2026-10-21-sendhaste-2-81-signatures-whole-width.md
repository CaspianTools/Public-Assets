Your profile, then Signatures, now uses the whole width of the screen: your addresses on the left, your signatures and the editor in the middle, and the live preview with the defaults on the right, so the preview stays in view while you edit. On smaller screens they stack, and on a phone you pick the address at the top.
Signatures show as a simple list, each marked with what it starts: New emails or Replies.
Plain text signatures from before now have the same lines as Main, with their text kept as a new "Your own text" line.
Details are in Help, under Writing and sending: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
