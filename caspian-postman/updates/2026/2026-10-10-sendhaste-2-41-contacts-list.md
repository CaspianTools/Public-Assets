The **Contacts** list is now grouped by who sees each contact, with **Company**, **Phone** and **Last email** columns and a **Sort** menu. After you upload contacts, a summary stays at the top with **Undo upload** in case the file was wrong. **Missing a name** lists contacts without a name, and you can now tick contacts to email them, set their relationship, add a tag or delete your own. **Add contact** sits next to **Upload contacts**, and on a phone there is a large **Add contact** button. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/contacts
