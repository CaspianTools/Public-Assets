Mail rules can now check up to three things at once, such as who it's from and whether it has an attachment, and can look at the text of the email. They can move mail to any of the mailbox's folders, and you can edit them and change their order. Org Super Admins can add **Organization rules** in **Settings** → **Rules** that run first in every mailbox. **Out of office** now takes times as well as days, can stay quiet on chosen weekdays, and can send colleagues their own reply. **Auto-archive** moves old emails from the Inbox to the Archive for you. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/labels-rules
