Your mail is yours to take with you. An organization's administrator can now open **Settings → Import & export → Download a mailbox**, pick a mailbox they can read and click **Download .zip**: every email comes as an .eml file, in one folder per mail folder, ready for mail apps and other mail services. Keep the page open while the bar fills; a large mailbox comes as several .zip files. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/move-mail
