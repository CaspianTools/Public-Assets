Every email you receive now shows a small tag next to the sender: "Verified sender" when it passed the checks of its sender's domain, "Unverified sender" when it did not, or "Not checked" when it carries no check results at all.
An email that claims to come from one of your own domains but arrived from another server without any sender checks is now held for review instead of reaching the Inbox, and shows the red warning when opened; until now only an email that failed the checks was.
The red warnings about fake senders now also appear on emails that claim to come from your own domains, not only on emails from outside.
Administrators can open "Show headers" under the sender to see the technical trail of an email and trace where a fake came from.
More in the help center: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/spam
