**Settings** → **Users** is easier to scan: each person is now one short row with their roles. Click a person to open their settings in a panel on the right, where you change their **Organization role** and click **Save**, see their **Domains**, or use **Sign-in**, **Send invitation** and **Remove**. **Add a user** and **Add many from a file** sit above the list. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/people-access
