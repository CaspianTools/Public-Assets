You can add a colleague to SendHaste with any email address they already use, not only a Google account. The invitation email now explains both ways in: **Continue with Google**, or **Create a password** for any other address. The help center's guide to adding a person with their own mailbox walks through each step, including how to check who can read the new mailbox: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/add-person
