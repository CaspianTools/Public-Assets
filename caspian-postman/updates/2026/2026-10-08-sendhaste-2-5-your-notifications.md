Everyone can now choose what they're notified about under the bell: open **Settings** → **Notifications**, turn off what you don't need and click **Save**. Each notification has its own switch, grouped under **Organization**, **Domains** and **Support**, and the organization settings for Org Super Admins (**Settings** → **Organization** → **Notifications**) work the same way. Anything your organization has turned off is shown greyed out. More in the help center: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/notifications
