When you open an email that is part of a conversation, the box at the top lists the other emails in it. Tapping one now shows that email right there, under its line, like in Gmail, instead of taking you to its folder; tap it again to fold it away, and Open this email inside it still goes to the email's own page.
Your sent replies stay in Sent Items as before; nothing moves.
The note under an email about blocked remote images is now a small card: the sentence on one line, with Load remote images and Always show pictures from … as buttons under it, which reads much better on a phone.
More in the help center: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/inbox
