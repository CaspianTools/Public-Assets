Long conversations scroll smoothly again: the mouse wheel no longer gets stuck inside each email.
With the reading pane open in a narrow window, the list and the pane no longer scroll sideways, and **Reply**, **Reply all** and **Forward** stay on one row.
An email's date and time are no longer cut off when the sender's name or address is long.
Opening Mail without choosing a mailbox now opens the one you read last, and replying to an email without a subject gives "Re: (no subject)".
