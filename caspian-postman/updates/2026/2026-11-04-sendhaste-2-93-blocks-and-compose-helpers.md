Saved blocks (the pieces of text you reuse in emails) are now made from the + at the top → "Text block", or "Add block" at the top of Your profile → Your blocks: give it a name, choose the domain it is for (or "Any domain") and write the text with a live preview.
Put your blocks in the order you like, by dragging one by its handle or with its "Move up" and "Move down" arrows; "Insert" in Compose lists them in that order, shows only the blocks for the domain you write from, and shows a block's whole text when you point at it.
In Compose, "From" is now a box you can type in: with several addresses, the list keeps only the ones that match what you typed.
"To", "Cc", "Bcc" and "Reply-to" suggest your contacts as you type; pick one with the arrow keys and Enter, or a click.
In a reply or forward, your signature now sits above the quoted email, right under your answer, instead of at the very bottom.
More in the help center: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
