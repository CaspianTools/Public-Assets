The editor in Compose has more formatting: undo and redo, a Text style (heading, subheading, quote or code), a Font and a Text size, strikethrough, a Highlight colour, indents, alignment, Remove link and a Horizontal line.
Right-click in the message for a formatting menu right where you point, with cut, copy and paste, styles, lists, alignment, colours and links. Hold Shift while you right-click for your browser's own menu.
When you pick a Template in Compose, you now see only the templates for the domain you write from, plus those with no domain.
Details are in Help, under Writing and sending: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
