Want your mail in Outlook, Apple Mail or your phone's mail app too? **Settings** → **Mail apps** now shows everyone the settings to type there, with the addresses of their own mailboxes. Every mailbox also has an **Archive** now, so you can clear an email out of the Inbox without deleting it. Org Super Admins can download any mailbox of their organization under **Settings** → **Import & export** → **Download a mailbox**, and each download is listed in **Activity**. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/mail-apps
