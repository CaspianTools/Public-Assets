The sendhaste.com website got a small clean-up for phones. The header is now the same blue as the welcome section below it, and it shows Sign in next to Request access on phones too, so existing customers can sign in from the top of any page. The header also stays at the top while you scroll, where it used to disappear after the first screen.

The footer on a phone is about half as long: Product and Support sit side by side, and the six languages are in one row.
