You can now give a team one address. On a domain's page in Settings → Domains, under **Team addresses**, create an address such as all@ or managers@ and tick its **Members**: every email sent to it arrives in each member's own Inbox, and replies go back to the sender. A team address can have up to 100 members. Forwarding also goes further: one email can now be forwarded to up to 9 addresses. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/connect-domain
