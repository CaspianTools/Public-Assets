A mailbox can now receive mail for more than one address. On a domain's page in **Settings → Domains**, type addresses such as billing@ or accounts@ in **Also receives** next to a mailbox and click **Save addresses**: their mail arrives in that mailbox. When writing, pick one of them in **Send as** to send from it, and replies go out from the address the email was sent to. Step by step: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
