Group emails now feel personal and play by the rules. Write {{first_name}}, {{company}} or {{email}} in the subject, preview text or message, with a fallback after a bar like {{first_name|there}}, and each person gets their own details. Every group email has an Unsubscribe link and one-click unsubscribe in mail apps; whoever unsubscribes is listed under Contacts → Unsubscribed and skipped from then on. Groups → Sent group emails shows who replied and who didn't, and Follow up writes to those who haven't. The template Checks tab now also warns about text left to fill in, spammy words, too many links and a missing Unsubscribe link. How it works: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/templates-groups.
