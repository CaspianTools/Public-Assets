A mailbox that was made with a password, without a Google account, can now get Google sign-in too. On the domain's page, open the mailbox under **Emails**, type a Google account (such as a Gmail) under **Google sign-in** and click **Connect**. From then on, **Continue with Google** with that account signs in as the mailbox, and the mailbox password keeps working. Org Super Admins and the domain's Domain Super Admins can do this, and the mailbox gets an email each time a Google account is connected. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/sign-in-security
