Compose now has a formatting bar: bold, italic and underlined text, a text colour, bulleted and numbered lists, links and pictures. Paste or drop a picture straight into your message and it's sent inside the email. Prefer plain text? Switch **Formatting** off on the bar, and SendHaste remembers it. Drafts keep their formatting and pictures. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
