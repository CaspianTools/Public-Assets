Everyone can now keep contacts of their own: open **Quick add** (+ in the header) and choose **Contact**. A new contact starts at **Only me**, so nobody else sees it, administrators included, and it's marked **Only you** in your list.

When a contact should be shared, **Who sees it** on the contact decides. An administrator of a domain can share it with **Everyone on** that domain, and an organization's administrator with **Everyone in the organization**. Contacts added automatically from your mail stay as they were: you see the ones your mailboxes wrote with.

More in [Contacts and companies](https://sendhaste.com/en/help/contacts).
