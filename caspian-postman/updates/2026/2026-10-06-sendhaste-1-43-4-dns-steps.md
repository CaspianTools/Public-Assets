On a domain's page in Settings → Domains, the MX record under "Step 2: receive mail here" used to stay open, marked "Required", even after mail had started arriving. Now, once step 2 shows "Done", its record folds away under "Show its 1 record" and is marked "Found", like step 1's records already were. Nothing to do on your side: keep your DNS records as they are. The step-by-step guide is at https://sendhaste.com/en/help/connect-domain.
