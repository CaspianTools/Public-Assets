A bell at the top of SendHaste now tells you when something changes for you: when you're added to an organization or a domain, when your role or mailboxes change, and when the SendHaste team answers your support ticket. A number on the bell shows how many you haven't seen yet, and clicking a notification takes you to where it happened. Opening the bell marks them read, and **See all notifications** (also in Settings → Notifications) shows the full list. More in the help center: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/notifications
