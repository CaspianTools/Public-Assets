The menu on the left now shows each mailbox by its name, with its domain under it in smaller text, so long addresses are no longer cut off. The highlight on the selected item, and on the **Mail**, **Contacts** and **Templates** buttons, is narrower, and the menu takes a little less room, leaving more space for your mail.
