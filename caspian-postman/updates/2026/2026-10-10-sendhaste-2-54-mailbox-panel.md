The list of emails on a domain is now easier to scan. In Settings → **Domains**, open a domain: under **Emails**, each address is one short row. Click it and its settings (**Sends as**, **Also receives**, **New password**, **Delete mailbox**) open in a panel on the right, with one **Save** button. **Create a mailbox** opens in the same kind of panel, from the button above the list. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/connect-domain
