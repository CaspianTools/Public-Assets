Caspian Mail now organizes everything around your organization: its people, its domains and the mailboxes on each domain, with Org and Domain roles that decide who can read and manage what. Teams can share an inbox properly: assign a conversation, mark it done, snooze it, star it and leave private notes for colleagues. Labels and inbox rules sort new mail for you and can send an out-of-office auto-reply on a schedule, and contacts whose email bounces are flagged so group emails skip them. You can also install Caspian Mail as an app, get notified about new mail, use Gmail-style keyboard shortcuts (press ? to see them) and, as an admin, review an activity log of every change.
