SendHaste now speaks Azerbaijani, Russian, Turkish, French and German as well as English. It follows your browser's language until you pick one in Settings → Language, and the language is part of every address, so a link you share opens in the same language. Your organization's admin pages (users, roles, domains, labels, inbox rules and activity) have moved into Settings, next to your own preferences. Mailboxes on our own mail server now sit in the mailbox menu in the header with all your other addresses.
