The sendhaste.com website has a new look. The welcome section is now a pale blue with dark text, or navy in dark mode, and the header matches it, with a Help link in the menu next to Features and Pricing.

The home page shows the product more clearly: a short list of what you keep when you switch, a bigger picture of a shared inbox, the three setup steps on one line, the most common questions in two columns with a "Still have a question?" card, and the price next to a slider that shows what it would cost for your team. At the bottom, existing customers find "Already a customer? Sign in".

In the footer, the language is now a dropdown next to the copyright line: pick one and the whole site switches.
