Templates now live in Settings: open Settings, then Templates in its sidebar, to see your templates or start one from the Gallery. On a phone, the bar at the bottom of the ☰ menu (Mail, Contacts and Settings) no longer covers the last items of the menu. The installed app now opens on a dark start screen with the SendHaste icon. Read more in Help: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/getting-started
