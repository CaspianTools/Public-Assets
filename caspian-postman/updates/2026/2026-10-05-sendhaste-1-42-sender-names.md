Your organization now sets everything about its domains and mailboxes itself, in Settings → Domains. New: next to each mailbox, "Sends as" sets the name its mail goes out with, for example "Sales team"; leave it empty and each person's own name is used. Our team no longer sets anything up for you behind the scenes: we watch over the service and help when you need us. The step-by-step guide is at https://sendhaste.com/en/help/connect-domain.
