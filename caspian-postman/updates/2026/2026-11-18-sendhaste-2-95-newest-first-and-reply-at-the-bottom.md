An open conversation now lists its emails with the newest on top and the first one at the bottom, so the latest reply is the first thing you see.
**Reply**, **Reply all** and **Forward** now stay at the bottom of an open email while you scroll, in the reading pane and on the email's own page, so you no longer have to scroll to the end of a long email to find them.
More in the help center: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/inbox
