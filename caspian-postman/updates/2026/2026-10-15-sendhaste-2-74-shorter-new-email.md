The New email window is now shorter: Cc, Bcc and Reply-to stay hidden until you need them. Click Cc, Bcc or Reply-to at the end of the To line to add that line, and click the × at its end to hide it again. A line that already has an address, such as Cc when you reply to all, is always shown.
Details are in Help, under Writing and sending: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
