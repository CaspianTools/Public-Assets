When you add someone in **Settings → Users → Add a user**, SendHaste can now email them for you. Leave **Send an invitation email** on, and they get a short email saying who added them and how to sign in with their Google account. If they reply, the reply comes to you.

Need to send it again? Click **Send invitation** next to them in **Users**. The full steps are in [Add a person with their own mailbox](https://sendhaste.com/en/help/add-person).
