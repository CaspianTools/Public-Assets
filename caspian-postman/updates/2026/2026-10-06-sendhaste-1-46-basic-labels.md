Every organization now starts with five labels to sort conversations: Urgent, Follow up, Invoices, Clients and Personal. They show under Labels in the inbox, and you can rename, recolour or delete them in Settings → Labels. On a domain's page in Settings → Domains, each mailbox now shows its storage as a bar and says who can read it. More at https://sendhaste.com/en/help/labels-rules.
