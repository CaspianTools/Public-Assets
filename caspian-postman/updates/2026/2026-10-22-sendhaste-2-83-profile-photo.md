You can now upload your photo in Your profile, under Details, and your organization's admin can upload the company logo under Company signature. Both are saved at once and made smaller for emails.
Each signature has a Picture choice: Your photo, Company logo or Initials. The picture now shows in the Classic and Compact layouts too.
Your profile is also easier to trust: phone numbers and links are checked as you type, Unsaved changes shows beside each Save button, leaving the page with unsaved changes asks first, and copying or deleting a signature or block asks before it replaces anything.
Saved blocks preview as you type and point out placeholders that won't fill in.
Step by step: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
