**Settings** → **Mail apps** has a new layout. Your mailboxes are listed on the left, with **Find a mailbox** when you have many. Click one to see the settings to type into Outlook, Apple Mail or a phone's mail app, each with a copy button. On an iPhone or iPad, scan the QR code with the camera to add the mailbox in one step; on a Mac, click **Download setup file**. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/mail-apps
