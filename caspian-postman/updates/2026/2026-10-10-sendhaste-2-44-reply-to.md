Want replies to go somewhere else, such as a team address? In Compose, click **Add Reply-to** under Bcc and type up to five addresses. Org Super Admins who allow forwarding outside the organization can now also set **Outside addresses per mailbox** in **Settings** → **Organization** → **Forwarding**. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
