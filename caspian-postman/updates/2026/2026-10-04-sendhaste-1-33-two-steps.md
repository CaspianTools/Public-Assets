Connecting your own domain to SendHaste now happens in two steps, so no incoming mail is lost while you set up. Step 1 adds three records that prove the domain is yours and let SendHaste send for it; as soon as they're found, the domain switches on and you can create its mailboxes. Step 2, the MX record, comes last: from that moment your incoming mail arrives in SendHaste, and the domain's page shows that it's receiving. Find it under Settings → Domains → your domain.
