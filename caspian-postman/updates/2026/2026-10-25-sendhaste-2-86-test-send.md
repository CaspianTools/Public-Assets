Before you send, click the arrow next to Send and choose Send a test to me: one copy comes to you, filled in as the first person in To will see it, while the email stays open.
Under the message, Compose now shows what merge tags such as {{first_name|there}} become for that person, and says when their contact has no name so the fallback is used.
After sending, the Sent message has a View message link to the email you just sent.
More in the help center: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
