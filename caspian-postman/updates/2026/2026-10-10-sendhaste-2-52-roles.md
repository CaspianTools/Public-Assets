Organization administrators can now decide who helps run SendHaste. In Settings → **Roles**, switch on what the **Org Member** role may do, such as **Users**, **Domains**, **Activity** or **Export**, and each opens that part of Settings. Under **New role**, make roles of your own, like Office manager, and give them to people in Settings → **Users**. Nobody can give a role that may do more than their own. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/people-access
