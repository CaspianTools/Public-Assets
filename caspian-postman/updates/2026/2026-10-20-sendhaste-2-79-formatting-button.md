When you write an email, the Formatting switch is now an Aa button in the bottom row, right after Send. It's lit while formatting is on; click it to switch to plain text and back.
The formatting bar above the message takes one line less, so you see more of what you're writing.
Details are in Help, under Writing and sending: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/writing
