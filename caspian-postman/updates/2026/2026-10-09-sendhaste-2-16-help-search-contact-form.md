The [help center](https://sendhaste.com/en/help) now has a search box. Type a few words in **Search the help center** and you'll see the guides that mention them, with your words highlighted and the best match first. The same box sits at the top of the menu beside every guide, and it works in all six languages.

**Contact us** at the bottom of the website now opens a short form instead of your email app. Write your name, your email and your message, click **Send message**, and we'll answer by email. Customers can keep using **Support** in the account menu to follow a ticket.
