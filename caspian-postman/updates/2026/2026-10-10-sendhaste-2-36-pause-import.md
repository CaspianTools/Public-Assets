While you bring mail into a mailbox (Settings → Import into a mailbox), there is now a **Pause** button next to **Stop**. Pause holds the import once the emails already on their way are in, and the button turns into **Resume**, which carries on from exactly where it paused. Keep the page open in between. **Stop** still ends the import, and running it again later skips the emails already imported. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/move-mail
