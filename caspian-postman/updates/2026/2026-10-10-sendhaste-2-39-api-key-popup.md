When you make a key in Settings → **API keys**, it now opens in its own window. The window shows the key's **Name** and the address it **Sends from**, so you know which website or app it is for. Click **Copy**, then **Done**, and the key is cleared from the page. More: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/api
