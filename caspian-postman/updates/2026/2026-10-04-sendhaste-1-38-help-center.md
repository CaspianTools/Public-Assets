SendHaste now has a public Help center at https://sendhaste.com/en/help, in all six languages. It has step-by-step guides for everyone on your team: reading and writing mail, assigning and snoozing conversations, labels and auto-replies, templates and group emails, contacts, and every keyboard shortcut. Administrators get a full walkthrough of connecting a domain: the DNS records in plain words, creating mailboxes, and moving your mail over without losing a single email. There's also a page on using Outlook, Apple Mail or a phone's mail app with your mailbox. Find Help in the website's menu, or in the app under your picture.
