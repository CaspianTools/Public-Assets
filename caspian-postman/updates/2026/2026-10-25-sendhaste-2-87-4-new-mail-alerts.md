New-mail alerts now reach your phone or computer even while SendHaste is closed. Turn them on in Settings, under Notifications and shortcuts, with Notify me about new mail.
That switch now stays on after you reload the page; before, it could look switched off although alerts kept coming.
Emails that bounce now mark the address as Undeliverable straight away.
Make an app password and Settings, then Mail apps, start with the mailbox you're reading in Mail, and a link to a section of Settings opens that section again.
More in the help center: https://sendhaste.com/en/help/notifications
