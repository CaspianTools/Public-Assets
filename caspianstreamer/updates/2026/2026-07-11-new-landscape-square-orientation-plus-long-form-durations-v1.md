## 🔄 Go landscape — and go longer

Not everything is a vertical short, so now the studio flexes to fit.

### Rotate the preview (and your video)

There's a new little **portrait / landscape / square** toggle right above the preview. Tap it and both the live preview **and your exported video** reshape instantly:

- **Portrait (9:16)** — Shorts, Reels, TikTok
- **Landscape (16:9)** — YouTube and desktop
- **Square (1:1)** — Instagram and LinkedIn feeds

Make a widescreen cut, a square feed post, and a vertical Short from the very same project — no starting over.

### Longer, long-form videos

The script step now goes beyond 60 seconds. Alongside the classic 15 / 30 / 60-second shorts, you can now draft **90 seconds, 2 minutes, or 3 minutes** — perfect for explainers and deep dives, and a natural fit for landscape.

Same clean, caption-first look — just more shapes and more room to tell your story. Everything still runs privately in your browser. Enjoy! 💜
