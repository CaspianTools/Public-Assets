## CaspianStreamer just got faster and lighter ✨

We spent this update making the whole studio feel smoother — from the moment you draft a script to the second your video finishes exporting. Nothing changed about how you use it; it just does more with less.

### What you'll notice

- **A buttery-smooth preview.** The live caption preview now plays without the little stutters some setups saw before. Scrubbing, play, and pause all feel crisp — even on modest laptops.
- **Faster, steadier exports.** Rendering your video is smoother and drops fewer frames, and exports now default to **crisp 1080p** — beautifully sharp for Reels, Shorts, and TikTok while rendering noticeably quicker. Prefer maximum resolution? **4K is still one click away**, and 720p is there for a speedy render.
- **Instant voice comparison.** Trying out the different AI voices is now instant — once you've generated a voice, switching back to it is immediate, with no waiting and no extra usage of your own API key.
- **What you preview is exactly what you get.** The preview and the exported file now share the very same rendering engine, so your captions look identical in the download.
- **More natural captions.** For pasted or hand-written scripts, short words flash by and longer words linger a touch — closer to how the line is actually spoken.
- **A snappier script editor.** Editing a long script stays responsive as you type.
- **Lighter and leaner.** The app loads a little quicker, and long sessions with lots of regenerating and re-exporting stay tidy instead of slowing down over time.

### Small helpful touches

- If the AI service is briefly busy, the Script and Voice steps now tell you it's retrying instead of just spinning.
- A gentle reminder on the Voice step: **Browser Speech is preview-only** — pick **Google AI Speech** if you want the voice included in the exported file.

As always, CaspianStreamer runs entirely in your browser — private, free, and yours. Enjoy the smoother ride, and let us know what you create! 🎬
