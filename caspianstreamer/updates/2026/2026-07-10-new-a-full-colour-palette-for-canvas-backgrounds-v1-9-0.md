## 🎨 A full colour palette for your video backgrounds

Choosing a solid background colour just got a whole lot better.

Until now, the **Background → Color** step gave you a small handful of dark shades. From this release, it opens with a clean, familiar palette — a full grayscale ramp from black to white, plus a bright, tasteful spread of reds, pinks, purples, blues, greens, yellows, and oranges. Everyday colours, right there at a glance.

### Need something more specific?

Tap **More colours** and the palette expands into the complete spectrum — every hue, from deep and dark through soft and light — in a neat scrollable grid. Found the shade you want? Great. Prefer to keep things simple? Tap again and it tucks back to the basics.

And if you already know the exact colour you're after, the **hex field** and the **screen eyedropper** are still right there — type a code or pick a colour from anywhere on your screen.

### Why you'll like it

- **Way more choice** — from subtle neutrals to bold, vivid tones.
- **Nothing in the way** — the essentials show first; the full range is one tap away.
- **Pixel-perfect when you need it** — exact hex and eyedropper picking are still built in.

Set the perfect backdrop for your captions in seconds. As always, everything runs privately in your browser — nothing leaves your device. Enjoy! 💜
