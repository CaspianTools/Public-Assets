We''ve given the app a glow-up — here''s what''s new. ✨

## A new name: Caspian Streamer
The app formerly known as "Minimalist Video Captioner" is now **Caspian Streamer** — a friendly member of the CaspianTools family of free, private, browser-based tools.

## Stay up to date — automatically
There''s now an **Updates** page (just tap the bell in the top bar). Caspian Streamer lets you know when a newer version is available and moves you onto it in a single click — no reinstalling, no hassle. A little red dot appears whenever there''s something new waiting, and you can turn automatic checks on or off.

## Everything a click away
A brand-new footer adds quick links to **Updates, Changelog, About, Privacy, and Contact**, so it''s easy to learn how the app works, see what''s changed, and get in touch. You''ll also find our **X** and **LinkedIn** there.

## New pages
- **About** — what Caspian Streamer is and how it keeps your work on your device.
- **Privacy** — a plain-language rundown of how your data is handled (short version: it stays with you).
- **Changelog** — every improvement, newest first.
- **Contact** — the fastest ways to reach us.

## A cleaner look
The whole interface now uses the **Poppins** typeface for a softer, more modern feel.

As always, Caspian Streamer runs entirely in your browser — private by design and free to use. Give the new update checker a spin and let us know what you think! 💬
