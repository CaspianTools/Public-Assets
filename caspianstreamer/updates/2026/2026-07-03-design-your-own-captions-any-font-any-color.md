Your captions, your way. 🎨 The Style step is now **Captions**, and instead of a handful of fixed presets, you're in full control of how your words look.

## Pick any font
A new **font picker** lets you choose from your device's own fonts plus a big selection of **Google Fonts** — sans-serif, serif, bold display, handwriting, and monospace styles. Whatever you pick loads instantly into the live preview, and it carries straight through to your final exported video.

## Choose your exact colors
Two simple color pickers let you set your **caption color** and your **highlight color** (the color of the word being spoken) to anything you like. No more settling for a preset — dial in the exact look that matches your vibe.

## A cleaner, smarter step rail
The step list on the right is lighter and less boxed-in now, so it blends naturally beside your work. It also follows your reading position more intuitively, highlighting each step as it reaches the middle of your screen. And the top bar is now solid, for a crisp, clutter-free scroll.

As always: **100% private, client-side, and free**, running entirely in your browser.

Go make your captions unmistakably yours! ✨
