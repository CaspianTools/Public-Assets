Good news for everyone creating videos with CaspianStreamer! 🎬

We've added an automated safety net that puts the app through its paces in a real browser before any update goes live. Every time we make a change, a suite of checks now walks through the entire creation journey — just like you would:

- Opening the app and getting set up with your key
- Drafting a script and watching the live caption preview appear
- Choosing your visual style, background, and voice
- Rendering and downloading a finished video

If anything along that path isn't behaving the way it should, we catch it *before* it ever reaches you.

**What this means for you**
- Fewer surprises and smoother updates
- More confidence that the features you rely on keep working release after release
- A faster path for us to ship improvements without accidentally breaking something

As always, CaspianStreamer runs entirely in your browser, and nothing about how you create your videos changes. This is purely behind-the-scenes work to keep your experience polished and dependable.

Thanks for creating with us! ✨
