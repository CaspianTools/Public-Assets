We just gave the studio a tidy-up so it's easier and calmer to work in — and added a proper guide for when you want a little help.

## One clear title per step
No more seeing the same step name twice. Each step now has a single, clean title, so you always know exactly where you are at a glance.

## Controls that breathe
We removed the boxed-in grey panels that used to wrap your sliders and dropdowns. Everything now sits right on the page under a small, tidy heading — closer to the polished feel of a modern design tool, and far less cluttered.

## A new How-To guide
Every "how does this work?" explanation now lives in one friendly place. The new **How To** guide walks you through all five steps — Script, Captions, Background, Audio, and Export — plus the credits for our built-in backgrounds. You can open it any time from the footer or the **Guide** link at the top of any step.

Don't worry — the tips that actually matter in the moment still show up right where you need them, like the reminder that browser voices preview here but won't be captured into your download.

## Same wholesome, private, in-browser experience
As always, everything runs entirely in your browser, your work never leaves your device, and our built-in backgrounds stay calm and people-free.

Refresh the app to get **v1.7.0**. We'd love to hear what you think! 💬
