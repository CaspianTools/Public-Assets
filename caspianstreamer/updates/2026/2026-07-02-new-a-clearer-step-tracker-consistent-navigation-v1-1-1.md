We've polished the way you move through the studio — the step tracker at the top of the wizard has a fresh, clearer look, and the navigation now speaks with one voice from top to bottom. 🎬

## A proper progress tracker
The five steps across the top are now a real progress tracker:

- **Your place, at a glance.** Each step's name sits above a numbered circle, with connecting lines linking them.
- **See what's done.** Finished steps turn green with a checkmark, the step you're on is highlighted, and a small **Completed / In Progress / Pending** label removes any guesswork.

## Navigation that always agrees
The buttons at the bottom of each step now name exactly the step they take you to — so the tracker up top and the buttons down below are always in sync. No more wondering where a button will actually send you.

## One clear name: "Audio"
The voice-over step is now simply called **Audio** — in the tracker, on the buttons, and in the step heading — so there's a single, consistent name throughout.

## Small fixes, tidier flow
The step numbers in the Audio and Export headings now line up correctly (4 and 5).

Everything still runs entirely in your browser — private, offline-friendly, and free. Update to **v1.1.1** and enjoy the smoother ride. We'd love to hear what you think!
