A big quality-of-life update just landed in the studio. Here's what's new in **v1.6.0**:

### 🎬 Backgrounds preview instantly
Pick an image or video background and it shows up in the live preview right away — no more nudging a blur or dim slider to make it appear.

### 🔤 A smarter caption font picker
- **Search** the whole font catalogue by name instead of scrolling.
- Your **recently used** fonts float to the top, so your favourites are always one tap away.
- New **weight** control — anything from Light to Black — plus an **italic** toggle.
- A **"Bolden the spoken word"** switch lets you decide: keep the karaoke-style emphasis where the word being spoken stands out heavier, or set one clean, uniform weight across the whole caption.

### ⚡ One-click "Re-render everything that changed"
The final step now shows exactly what you've changed since your last render — script, captions, background, or audio — and rebuilds your video in a single click. Changed your topic? It offers to re-draft the script first, and always checks with you before replacing anything you've edited.

---

As always, everything runs entirely in your own browser — private, and free. Update when you're ready and enjoy the smoother flow ✨
