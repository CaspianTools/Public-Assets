The studio just got a lot calmer and a lot more helpful. Here's what's new. ✨

## One step at a time 🎯
Each step now fills the screen as a single, tidy card — no more half-shown cards peeking above and below. Clear **Back** and **Next** buttons sit right at the bottom of every card, so moving through Script → Captions → Background → Audio → Export feels effortless. The step rail on the right is neatly centered and out of your way.

## See your captions before you write a word 👀
Open the **Captions** step and you'll now see a live sample caption in the preview. Change the font, colors, capitalization, size, or the ambient glow, and it restyles **instantly** — so you can perfect your look first, then drop in your script and watch it take over.

## Pick fonts you can actually see 🔤
The font menu now shows every typeface **in its own style**, neatly grouped into your device fonts and a broad Google Fonts selection. What you see is what you'll get — in both the preview and your final export.

Everything still runs privately in your browser, no account required. Give it a spin and let us know what you create! 🎬
