Got a script already written? This update makes dropping it in feel effortless.

## Pasting your own script, cleaned up ✨

When you choose **Paste your own**, type in your script, and tap **Use This Script**, three things are nicer now:

- **One tidy editor, not two.** The paste box neatly tucks itself away into a single "Script Ready" editor — no more seeing the same words in two places at once.
- **You're carried forward.** The studio gently scrolls you on to the **Captions** step, so it's clear your script was accepted and you know exactly what to do next.
- **Nothing gets lost.** Switching over to *Paste your own* keeps whatever script you already had until you actually replace it. Changed your mind? A small **"Paste a different script"** link lets you swap in another one anytime.

As always, pasting your own script needs **no API key** — your words go straight to word-by-word captions, all privately in your browser.

Thanks for streaming with us. More small polish on the way! 🎬
