Backgrounds just got a big, delightful upgrade in **CaspianStreamer v1.2.0** — and the highlight is a beautiful new collection that lives right inside the app.

## ✨ Introducing Caspian Originals

We've hand-picked a small, gorgeous collection of backgrounds and built them **right into the studio**, so they:

- **Load instantly** — nothing to fetch, no waiting.
- **Work with no internet** — pick a background on the train, on a plane, anywhere.
- **Never break** — they're part of the app, so they're always there.

The starter set is calm, wholesome, and made for focused short-form video:

- 🏜️ **Desert Dunes** — sweeping sand ripples seen from orbit
- 🌌 **Crab Nebula** — deep-space colour from the Hubble telescope
- 🌲 **Misty Forest** — a quiet, foggy woodland
- 🌅 **Serene Sea** — a golden calm-water sunrise
- ❄️ **Quiet Snowfall** — soft falling snow (a gentle moving clip)
- 🌠 **Starlit Clouds** — a starry night drifting over the desert (a moving clip)

## 🗂️ A clearer, tabbed background picker

Choosing a background is now simpler. Everything is organized into clean tabs:

**Solid Color · Caspian Originals · Stock Images · Stock Videos · Custom URL**

Jump straight to what you want — no more scrolling through a mixed list.

## 🎨 A proper colour picker

The **Solid Color** tab now has quick colour swatches *and* a full colour picker, right where you'd expect them, so dialing in the perfect minimalist backdrop takes seconds.

## 🛠️ Smoother and more reliable

- Refreshed a couple of stock images so the picker always looks complete.
- The preview's little "Voice Syncing" badge is now more honest — it only shows when your narration is truly playing, and the app will gently remind you to check your tab and system volume if you don't hear anything.
- Chosen backgrounds are more dependable and less likely to hiccup.
