## ✍️ Better long-form drafts — even without an API key

A quick follow-up to the new long-form durations.

If you draft a script **without adding your own Gemini key**, the built-in offline writer just got a lot more to say. It now draws on much deeper, on-tone sentence sets for every style — Stoic, Educational, Calm, Minimalist, and Motivational — so your **90-second, 2-minute, and 3-minute** scripts come out with real substance instead of a few lines stretched to fill the time.

Prefer to use your own AI key? Nothing changes there — your scripts are still written fresh and full-length every time.

Same private, in-browser workflow. Enjoy the longer stories! 💜
