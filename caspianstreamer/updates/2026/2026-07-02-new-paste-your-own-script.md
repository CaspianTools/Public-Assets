Big news for anyone who already writes their own scripts: **you can now paste your own script straight into CaspianStreamer.** ✍️

Until now, the first step always started with a topic and let Gemini draft the words for you. That is still there — but on the **Draft Your Script** step you will now see a simple choice:

- **Generate with AI** — give a topic and let the AI write a perfectly timed spoken script (just like before).
- **Paste your own** — already have a script? Drop it in and we take it from there.

### Why you will love it
- **Use the words you want.** Wrote something by hand, or drafted it somewhere else? Paste it in and keep full creative control.
- **Instant, automatic captions.** However your script arrives, CaspianStreamer times it **word by word** at a natural reading pace with gentle pauses at punctuation — so your captions stay perfectly in sync.
- **No key needed for pasting.** The paste option works without any API key at all. You only need a key when you ask the AI to write or voice something for you.
- **Edit anytime.** Pasted scripts land in the same editable box as AI drafts, so you can tweak a line before moving on to styling, voice, and export.

### How to use it
1. Open the **Draft Your Script** step.
2. Tap **Paste your own**.
3. Paste your script and hit **Use This Script** — your captioned preview comes to life instantly.

As always, please keep your scripts wholesome and family-friendly. Enjoy the extra freedom, and happy creating! 🎬
