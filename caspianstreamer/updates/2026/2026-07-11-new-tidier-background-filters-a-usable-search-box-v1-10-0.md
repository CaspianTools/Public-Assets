## 🔎 Tidier background filters — and a search box you can actually type in

Picking the perfect Image or Video background is quicker now.

### Filters are dropdowns

The row of filter chips that used to wrap across the screen is gone. **Availability**, **Source**, and **Category** are now neat little dropdown menus. Open one, start typing to jump straight to what you want, and pick it — no more hunting through a wall of pills.

- **Availability** — built-in offline library, live online sources, or both.
- **Source** — browse everything, or just Unsplash, Wikimedia, NASA, or Pexels.
- **Category** — Nature, Lo-Fi, Minimalist, Philosophy, Cyberpunk, or all of them.

### A real search field

The background search box is now full width and comfortable to type in. Search any Image or Video by name or mood and the grid narrows instantly.

Cleaner, faster, and a lot less scrolling. As always, everything runs privately in your browser. Enjoy! 💜
