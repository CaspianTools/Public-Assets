We've given the Caspian Streamer studio a fresh, roomier layout — and it makes building a video feel a whole lot smoother. ✨

## Everything on one page
Your five steps — **Script, Style, Background, Audio, and Export** — now live together as clean, clearly-separated cards. Scroll straight down through your whole video from first idea to final render, without ever losing your place or clicking back and forth.

## A handy step rail on the right
A new step list runs down the right edge of the studio. It shows exactly where you are, marks the steps you've finished, and lets you **jump straight to any step with a single click** — the page glides right to it. As you scroll, the rail keeps up and highlights the step you're on.

## A bigger, screen-filling preview
Your live preview now **stretches to fill the height of your screen** instead of sitting in a small fixed box. Your captions look larger and crisper while you work, and the preview stays pinned in view as you move through the steps — so you can always see your changes come to life.

## Same great everything else
It's still **100% private and client-side**, still free, and still runs entirely in your browser. Nothing about your workflow changes — there's just more room to breathe and a faster way to move around.

Give it a try, and let us know what you think! 💬
