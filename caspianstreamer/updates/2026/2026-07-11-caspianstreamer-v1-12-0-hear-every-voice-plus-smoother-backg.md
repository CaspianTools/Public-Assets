Say hello to **CaspianStreamer v1.12.0** — three upgrades that make building your video smoother from the first click.

## 🎧 Meet your narrator before you commit

Choosing an AI voice used to be a guessing game. Now every voice persona clearly shows whether it sounds **male or female**, alongside a short description of its style. Even better: each one has a **one-tap listen button** so you can hear a quick sample and pick the perfect narrator by ear — no need to generate a full track just to audition a voice.

## ⚡ Video backgrounds show up instantly

Pick a video background and it now appears in the preview **right away**. Previously you had to click between steps to nudge it into view — that's fixed. What you choose is what you see, immediately.

## 🎨 Every caption colour is now clickable

The Caption and Highlight colour palettes now stay fully on screen, so **every swatch is reliably selectable** — including the ones on the right edge that could previously be hard to click. Styling your captions just got more predictable.

---

As always, CaspianStreamer runs entirely in your browser — your topic in, a polished captioned short out, with nothing to install.

Enjoy, and keep the feedback coming! 💬
