A small but handy quality-of-life update: **CaspianStreamer v1.12.1** makes the "Update available" notification show up much faster.

## 🔔 Know about new versions within minutes

Previously, an open tab only looked for a new release once a day — so if a fresh version went live, you might not see the little update bell light up until much later. Now the app quietly checks in the background **and the moment you switch back to the tab**, so a new version surfaces within minutes of release.

Nothing to do on your end — when an update is ready, you'll see the bell, and one click loads the latest build. As always, the manual **Check for updates** button on the Updates page gives you the newest version instantly whenever you want it.
