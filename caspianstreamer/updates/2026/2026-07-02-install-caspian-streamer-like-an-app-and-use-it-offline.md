## Install Caspian Streamer like an app — and use it offline 🎬

Caspian Streamer is now a fully **installable app**. Instead of keeping a browser tab open, you can add it to your phone's home screen or your desktop and open it in its own clean, focused window — no address bar, no clutter, just the studio.

### How to install

- **Desktop (Chrome / Edge):** Look for the new **Install** button in the top bar, or the install icon in your browser's address bar. One click and it lives in your dock/taskbar.
- **Android:** Open the menu and choose **Install app** / **Add to Home screen** (or tap the in-app Install button when it appears).
- **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. It launches full-screen with its own icon.

Once installed, it opens like any other app, with a crisp icon and a standalone window.

### Now works offline

The studio itself now loads even when you're offline, so you can open it anytime. The background image or clip you picked is remembered too, so a project you were working on still previews without a connection.

A quick note: drafting scripts and generating AI voices still needs the internet, since those use your own AI key — but everything else keeps working offline.

### Same privacy, still 100% in your browser

Nothing about how Caspian Streamer works has changed under the hood: it's still completely client-side, your key stays in your browser, and your videos are made entirely on your own device. Installing it just gives you a faster, app-like way in.

Give it a try — tap **Install** and let us know how it feels. 🚀
