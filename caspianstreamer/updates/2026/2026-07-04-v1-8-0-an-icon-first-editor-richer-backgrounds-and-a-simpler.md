CaspianStreamer just got clearer to read and quicker to use. Here's what's new in **v1.8.0**.

## Controls you recognise at a glance
No more hunting through dropdowns. **Italic**, **Bold**, **vertical alignment**, and **capitalization** are now single-tap icon buttons — the kind you already know from every design tool. Pick a look and see it instantly.

## A proper colour picker
Caption, highlight, and background colours now open a clean picker with **preset swatches**, a **hex field** you can type into, and a **screen eyedropper** to grab any colour you like. Getting the exact shade you want is finally effortless.

## A bigger, smarter background library
The background step is now simply **Color**, **Image**, or **Video** — and each one lets you choose between the built-in **Offline** library, live **Online** sources, or **Both** together.

There's a lot more to browse, too: beautiful, people-free imagery from **NASA** and **Pexels** now joins the existing Wikimedia and Unsplash collections. Want to see just one source? A quick **Source** filter lets you browse Wikimedia, Unsplash, Pexels, or NASA on their own.

## Try your voice for free — before you generate
The audio step is simpler and kinder to your budget. A new **Try offline** button previews the pacing and feel using your device's own voice at **no cost** — so you only spend on the high-quality AI voice once it sounds right.

And you now have real control over the delivery: **Speed**, **Pitch**, and a **Tone** like Calm, Energetic, or Authoritative. Dial it in, hear it in the free preview, then generate.

## A cleaner, icon-first look
Decorative emojis across the studio have been replaced with crisp, consistent icons — a small change that makes the whole editor feel calmer and more polished.

---

Everything still runs entirely in your browser, private and free. Open the studio, tweak a few icons, pick a background, and press play. Enjoy! 🌿
