Getting into Zekio just got easier, and the app finally looks like itself on your home screen.

## Forgot your password? You can now reset it

The link on the sign-in screen finally does something. Type in your email address, tap send, and Zekio sends you a link to set a new password. No more being locked out of your own practice tests.

## Sign up with Google

The Google button on the sign-up screen now works, exactly like the one on the sign-in screen. Pick your account and you go straight into setting up your profile — no password to invent, no email to confirm. Signing up takes about five seconds.

## A real app icon

Zekio on your home screen is now the Zekio open-book mark — the same one you see on the web. It replaces the plain blue placeholder circle the app had been shipping with. It also has a proper themed version, so on Android 13 and newer it picks up your wallpaper colours along with the rest of your icons.

## Tidier sign-in

A few buttons that never did anything are gone, and the ones that remain now say something useful when they cannot help — if there is no Google account on your phone, Zekio tells you so instead of shrugging.

Happy studying, and good luck with LGS.
