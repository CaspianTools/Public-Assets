We just shipped a big home-screen update. 🎉

**Every test now works offline.** Your tests are built right into the app, so they open instantly — no waiting, no connection needed. Whenever we add new tests, they arrive with your next app update.

**Three tabs to find exactly what you want**
- **All** — your full library of tests
- **Favorites** — the ones you've saved
- **New** — the latest tests we've added

**Save your favorites** ⭐ Tap the star on any test to save it. Your favorites stay put when you reopen the app and even follow you to a new device.

**Filters, front and center.** Grade, subject, category, and difficulty chips now sit right on the home screen — tap to narrow things down in a second. And the first time you open the app, we pre-select filters that match you, so you land on tests that fit right away.

Happy studying! 📚
