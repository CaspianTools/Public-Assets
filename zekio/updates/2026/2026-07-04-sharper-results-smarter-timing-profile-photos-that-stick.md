A few refinements that make your quiz sessions clearer and more personal. ✨

## 📊 A clearer results breakdown
Your results now separate every question into **Correct**, **Wrong**, and **Skipped** — so the ones you skip are counted on their own instead of dragging down your "wrong" tally. The numbers finally add up the way you'd expect.

## ⏱️ Duration that matches your settings
The **Duration** shown on a quiz now reflects *your* Time-per-question setting (and whatever you choose for that session), and it updates live as you adjust it — no more guessing how long a quiz will really take.

## 🖼️ Profile photos that stick
Set a profile picture and it stays put — after you reopen the app, and even on a new device.

## ✨ A little polish
The **Home** and **Retry** buttons on your results screen now have icons for quicker recognition.

Update and enjoy the cleaner ride! 💙
