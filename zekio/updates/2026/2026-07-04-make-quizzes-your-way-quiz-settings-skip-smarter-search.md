We just shipped a set of quality-of-life upgrades that put **you** in control of how you take quizzes. 🎯

## ⚙️ New Quiz Settings, right in your Profile
- **When to see answers** — reveal each answer *immediately* as you go, only *at the end* of the quiz, or *both*. Study the way that works best for you.
- **Time per question** — set your own countdown (15–120 seconds) that applies across every quiz, or keep each quiz's own timer with **Default**.

## ⏭️ Skip any question
Stuck on a tricky one? A **Skip** button is now always there, so you can keep your momentum and move on.

## 🔎 Smarter dropdowns everywhere
Every dropdown — language, country, grade and the new settings — is now sorted alphabetically, and once there are more than a handful of choices, just start typing to filter them instantly. Finding your language among 100+ options is now a breeze.

Update and give it a try — your quizzes, your rules. 💪
