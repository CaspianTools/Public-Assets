Zekio 2.0 brings the fresh new look to the Android app. Everything we designed over the last few releases is now on your phone, screen for screen.

**A cleaner home**
- One list of tests with a search box, a quick *Favorilerim* toggle and a filter sheet for subject, category, level and grade.
- New tests wear a green *YENİ* badge, and tests you've already downloaded are marked.
- No more bottom bar: tap your avatar for your profile, the bell for new tests, and the menu for *Sıralama*, *Geçmiş*, *Bildirimler* and *Hakkında*.

**Taking a test feels better**
- A clear question card, lettered answers and the explanation right after each answer.
- Every new question starts at the top, so you never land halfway down the page.
- The result screen shows your score ring, correct / wrong / skipped counts, how you did against your best, your topic skills and a full answer key. The share card looks great too.

**Your profile, tidied up**
- Your stats and latest tests up top, fun emoji avatars, and all settings in neat cards.
- Your date of birth is picked from simple day / month / year lists.

**Light and dark, done right**
- The colours match the web version in both themes, whatever your phone's wallpaper is.

Also fixed: avatars now show as emoji, your history and stats appear right after signing in, the leaderboard marks your own scores, and your onboarding answers and settings always stick.

Keep practising, and good luck with LGS!
