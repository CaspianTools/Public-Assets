Small polish, big clarity: when you pick where you're from during onboarding — and on your profile — the picker now shows the **full country name** next to the flag.

Before, you saw just the two-letter code like "🇹🇷 TR". Now you see "🇹🇷 Türkiye", "🇬🇧 United Kingdom", "🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia", and so on. No more guessing what a code means — just pick your country and go.

Thanks for helping us make Zekio a little friendlier, one detail at a time. 💙
