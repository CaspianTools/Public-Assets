**Say hello to Zekio's release notes! 🎉**

We're kicking off a public changelog so you can always see what's new in **Zekio** — the quiz app that turns studying into a game.

### What Zekio gives you today
- 🧠 **Browse & search** a growing library of quizzes, with quick filters by category, language, country, grade, subject, and difficulty.
- ✍️ **Three question styles** — single choice, multiple choice, and true/false — with rich images and tap-to-zoom.
- ⏱️ **Optional per-question timer** to keep you on your toes.
- 🏅 **Instant A+-to-F grading**, a full answer review after every quiz, and unlockable badges & avatars as you improve.
- 🏆 **Leaderboards** — global, weekly, and monthly — plus your own personal attempt history.
- 🌍 **Five languages** (English, Turkish, Arabic, French, German) and a gorgeous light & dark theme.

### What's new — v1.0.1
We've started keeping a **public changelog**, so every update from here on will be shared right here in Announcements. Follow along to catch each new feature the moment it lands. 🚀

Thanks for being part of the Zekio journey — more good stuff is on the way! 💛
