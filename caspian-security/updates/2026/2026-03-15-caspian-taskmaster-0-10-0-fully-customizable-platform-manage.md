Caspian Taskmaster 0.10.0 is here — platforms are no longer hardcoded.

🎯 **What's new:**
- 16 default platforms out of the box (iOS, Android, Web, Desktop, Cloud, CLI, and more)
- Enable/disable platforms without deleting them
- Create your own custom platforms to match your stack
- New Platforms tab in the management modal
- Gear button on the toolbar for quick access

Your existing issues keep working — legacy platform values stay editable even after customization.
