Caspian Taskmaster 0.16 is here — you can now attach images directly to any issue.

- 🖼️ Images render inline in markdown preview
- 🗂️ Thumbnail gallery with one-click removal
- 📦 Smart storage — images kept as separate files, backed up on uninstall
- ⚡ 2 MB per image, 10 per issue — keeps things fast
