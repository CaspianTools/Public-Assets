Caspian Taskmaster 0.16.3 adds multi-select status filtering to the toolbar.

- Select multiple statuses with checkboxes (e.g. "New" + "Ongoing" together)
- Trigger button shows "All statuses" or "N of 5" summary
- Saved views fully support the new format with backward compatibility
- Escape key and click-outside close the dropdown
