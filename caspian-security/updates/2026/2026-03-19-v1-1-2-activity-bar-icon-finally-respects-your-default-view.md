Caspian Taskmaster 1.1.2 ships a fix that makes the Activity Bar icon actually do what your settings say.

- Clicking the icon now opens the tracker in your configured **Default View** (Full Tracker tab, Sidebar, or Panel)
- Previously the icon always forced the sidebar open regardless of your setting
- Startup is unaffected — no unexpected panels opening when VS Code loads

Change your preferred view under Settings → Default View and the icon will follow.
