Caspian Security 9.0 is here — the biggest UX and reliability update yet.

- Security Score in the status bar (0-100, graded A-F) so you always know where you stand
- First-run Welcome panel that scans your workspace and shows your posture instantly
- Triage Mode walks you through every finding one by one: AI Fix, Ignore, or False Positive
- PR-scoped scanning: only check files changed on your branch vs main
- Bulk "Ignore All" and "Why?" buttons on every finding
- 230 unit tests validating all 164 rules, the ignore system, and scoring
- GitHub Actions CI pipeline: lint, compile, test on every PR
