Caspian Taskmaster 0.6 is here — you can now create, edit, and delete Sprints and Milestones directly from the issue modal.

- Click the gear icon next to any Sprint or Milestone dropdown to open the management modal
- Create sprints with date ranges and goals for time-boxed planning
- Create milestones with deadlines and descriptions for release tracking
- Two-tab modal (Sprints / Milestones) layers above the issue modal — manage entities mid-edit
